@@b2304 h2
Spread Triples (2023)

@@b2305 p
In this study I look at Spread Triples: lines that connect three
Return points. I show how to draw them, what the spacing between the
Returns tells us about selling, how the lines work as a magnet for
Returns, and how they measure the pace of Propagation. I finish with a
full story on CADCHF.

@@b2306 h3
What a Spread Triple Is

@@b2307 p
A Spread Triple is the connection of three Return points.

@@b2309 p
Figure: Three intermediate Returns, A, B and C, joined by Spread
Triple lines as the market spreads lower. Real data: EURJPY, 4-hour, 14
Feb – 4 Mar 2022. (The original chart was AUDCHF weekly, Oct 2020 – Jul
2023; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b2310 p
Here we have three intermediate Returns, A, B and C, that produce
several weeks of down movement. These lines show how the 1-2-3 spreads.
The best approach is to draw them only on intermediate and major Return
points. That filters out any minor moves.

@@b2311 h3
Measuring the Rate of Spread

@@b2312 p
Now put a dot on each Return as it falls, vertically — like measuring
the speed of a ball falling to earth.

@@b2314 p
Figure: A dot on each Return, A, B and C, to measure how fast the
Returns fall. Real data: EURJPY, 4-hour, 14 Feb – 4 Mar 2022. (The
original chart was AUDCHF weekly, Oct 2020 – Jul 2023; this one shows
the same structure.)

@@b2315 p
Do these points tell us something? Do they always have a rate of
spread? My reading:

@@b2316 ul
<li><p>The farther apart these points are, the more selling there is in
the market.</p></li>
<li><p>The closer together they are, the less selling there is.</p></li>

@@b2318 p
Figure: Three Returns on one Spread Triple line are critical points;
when price exceeds them, watch for a new Return around them. Real data:
EURNZD, 1-hour, 14 Feb – 4 Mar 2022. (The original chart was GBPCAD
weekly, Oct 2017 – May 2023; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b2319 p
These points are also very critical for the market. When price
exceeds them, watch for a new Return around them.

@@b2321 p
Figure: After price exceeds the red line, new black lines are drawn
for the new sequence of counter Returns. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 22
Feb – 1 Mar 2022. (The original chart was USDCAD weekly, Aug 2019 – Jun
2023; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b2322 p
When price exceeds the red line, draw new lines for the new sequence
of Returns, as with the black line.

@@b2323 h3
Lines That Set the Pace
of Propagation

@@b2324 p
One advantage of these lines is that they define the pace of the
Returns that make the Propagation.

@@b2326 p
Figure: The red Spread Triple line marks short Setups and the green
line marks long Setups. Real data: NZDUSD, 1-hour, 23 Feb – 4 Mar 2022.
(The original chart was EURSGD weekly, Oct 2020 – Jul 2023; this one
shows the same structure.)

@@b2327 p
The red line marks high-probability short Setups, and the green line
marks high-probability long Setups. The logic is simple:

@@b2328 ul
<li><p>Along the green line there is no 1-2-3 down.</p></li>
<li><p>Along the red line there is no 1-2-3 up, so any long Setup will
fail.</p></li>

@@b2329 h4
The magnet for Returns

@@b2330 p
These lines also usually act as a magnet for Returns. The Returns are
drawn to them when they meet the TP.

@@b2332 p
Figure: Each Return reaches the TP of the leg before it and stops on
the Spread Triple line ("Spread + TP"); the last Return fails to reach
its TP. Real data: EURUSD, 4-hour, 14 Feb – 4 Mar 2022. (The original
chart was NZDUSD weekly, Oct 2020 – Jul 2023; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b2333 p
These TPs tell the story when they meet the Spread Triple lines. At
the TP the sellers lose 50% and the buyers gain 50%. The sellers must
also keep their pace of Return; the buyers must not exceed it.

@@b2335 p
Figure: Buyers exceed the orange line; the first counter Return holds
above the 50% TP of the first leg up, and buying Propagation grows. Real
data: USDJPY, 1-hour, 21–28 Feb 2022. (The original chart was USDJPY
weekly, Oct 2017 – May 2023; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b2336 p
Here the sellers are not willing to increase their offers. The first
Return occurs when price exceeds the line, and then buying Propagation
grows larger and larger. "Knowing others is wisdom."

@@b2337 h3
Adding the EP Line of the
Propagation

@@b2338 p
Next, draw a line on the EP of the Propagation after the Spread
Triple Return line.

@@b2340 p
Figure: The Spread Triple Return lines before the EP line is added.
Real data: GBPJPY, 4-hour, 14 Feb – 2 Mar 2022. (The original chart was
AUDCHF weekly, Oct 2020 – Jul 2023; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b2341 p
These lines represent the pace of the Return. Now add the EP of the
Propagation. This is NOT a channel. It is a free EP drawing, without
caring about the angle of the EP-of-SS line. Try to find the best fit to
the story.

@@b2343 p
Figure: The green EP line added below, where sellers lose their
power. Real data: GBPJPY, 4-hour, 14 Feb – 2 Mar 2022. (The original
chart was AUDCHF weekly, Oct 2020 – Jul 2023; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b2344 p
The green line shows where sellers lose their power, reach
equilibrium, and the market bounces back to the Return. The concept is
that the market moves from equilibrium to disequilibrium, and so on.

@@b2346 p
Figure: A down Propagation framed by two freely drawn lines: the
Return line (the pace of sellers) and the EP line where sellers lose
power.

A Full Story (first read on
CADCHF)

@@b2360 p
Figure: The red Return line above and the green EP line below, with
the current price at the EP of the Propagation. Real data: EURJPY,
1-hour, 15 Feb – 4 Mar 2022. (The original chart was CADCHF weekly, Aug
2015 – Apr 2023; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b2362 p
Figure: Points A to F: the EP at A, the Return at B, the red Return
line, D speeding up, E breaking the pace, and F meeting the line through
A and C. Real data: USDJPY, 1-hour, 14–24 Feb 2022. (The original showed
CADCHF H4 and weekly side by side, 2019–2023, with the G and H lines on
the H4; this one shows the weekly A-to-F structure on one chart.)

@@b2363 h4
The weekly picture

@@b2364 blockquote
<p>1. The story starts with the EP of the Propagation at A. It ends with
a wide spread and an abnormal surge in volume.</p>
<p>2. The Return comes with a cancel at B, the EP of the SS. New selling
arrives, shown by a wide-spread reversal bar with abnormal volume. No
buyer is willing to pay a higher price at B.</p>
<p>3. Selling dominates the trend along the red Spread Triple line.
Sellers are able to propagate lower, and no one is interested in taking
their offers.</p>
<p>4. At D the pace of Propagation speeds up, but buyers break the pace
at E.</p>
<p>5. Price re-propagates from E along the red line — the Return line —
trying to make new lows at F. F meets the Matrix Overlay exactly, at the
EP of the Propagation at C and A. The market is now in hesitation.</p>

@@b2365 h4
The H4 picture

@@b2366 p
On the lower time frame, H4, we have the Spread Triple line G and the
EP-of-Propagation line H. The black circles show that sellers fail to
make new lows, and buyers are willing to take the offers. The quality of
these buyers comes from three sources:

@@b2367 blockquote
<p>1. A, or the Matrix Overlay.</p>
<p>2. Sellers who shorted at B and now have a good profit to take.</p>
<p>3. Buyers who profited from the last Propagation and now want to
re-enter the trade.</p>

@@b2368 h4
The real test

@@b2369 p
When the buyers gain more quality and break the G line, expect a
selling reaction. The sellers will also try, from the E Spread Triple
line, to continue the Propagation lower. This will be the real test of
whether the buyers can make a genuine move.
