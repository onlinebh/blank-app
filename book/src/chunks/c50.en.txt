ETCUSD Study (2021)

@@b3147 p
This short study reads the volume at the top of an ETCUSD rally. I
want to show how a few bars, read together with their volume, tell us
that the buyers' energy is ending.

@@b3149 p

volume marked below. Real data: NZDUSD, 2-hour, 17–25 February 2022.
(The original chart was ETCUSD, timeframe and dates not known; this one

@@b3150 h3
Reading the Bars

@@b3151 ul
<li><p><strong>At A:</strong> this volume can stop all the buyers'
energy. I consider it a very definitive sign of reversal.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At B:</strong> it confirms the sign at A. Volume is
decreasing, and buyers are losing energy.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At D:</strong> for the first time, sellers increased the
size of the bar. This added to the sellers' energy.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>The last bar:</strong> a lower low and a lower high. It
does not reveal any danger to the sellers' energy.</p></li>

@@b3152 h3
Conclusion

@@b3153 p

pass the top at A.

The GBPUSD Studies (2021–2022)

@@b3172 p
Between October 2021 and January 2022 I ran a running study of one
market, GBPUSD, and sent each part to Michael as I wrote it. Most weeks
there was a new number. Some studies are long; some are only a chart and
a few lines written while the market was still moving.

@@b3173 p
The purpose was never to forecast. It was to practice reading, in
public, the struggle between buyers and sellers through the lens of
Goodman: every 1-2-3, every Propagation, every bar of volume. I kept the
studies in order so that you can see the market change its mind, and see
me change mine with it. For this book each chart is redrawn on real 2022
data with the same structure, often on a shorter time frame; when I name
the daily or the 4-hour, or give a date, I mean my original chart.

@@b3174 h3
Study 1 (late October 2021)

@@b3175 p
GBPUSD was moving sideways between an Excess line above and a Test
line below. My question was simple: who is losing energy inside this
range?

@@b3177 p
Figure: Buyers drive the price up, then it swings A–E between the
Excess line and the Test line. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 15–21 February
2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD, 4-hour, 12–26 October 2021; this
one shows the same structure.)

@@b3178 ul
<li><p><strong>At A</strong>, sellers stopped on increasing volume. They
lost their energy before they could move lower than A, and the Test line
held their attack.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At C</strong>, sellers' energy was very low, as the
shrinking volume shows.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At D</strong>, there was a small Propagation, but buyers
stopped below the Excess line. They lost their chance to move higher
than last time.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At E</strong>, sellers came again on low volume, and they
held higher than C.</p></li>

@@b3179 p
In summary, sellers failed to pass the Test line, and buyers could
not yet jump the Excess line. E may be the last test where sellers could
break the Test line. Yet their intention was fading, as the volume at A,
B, C and E shows. So, relatively, buyers were stronger than sellers at
these levels.

@@b3180 p
On the 15-minute chart there was a potential Return Zone setup.

@@b3182 p
Figure: After a fall, A, the Excess line and the test of A at B form
a small range. Real data: GBPUSD, 5-minute, 4 March 2022. (The original
chart was GBPUSD, 15-minute; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3183 p
At A we had a Jumbo. At B, the angle of the sellers' leg, compared
with A, showed that sellers emotionally did not want to push lower, and
the volume at B confirmed it. At that moment I could easily say buyers
had more power than sellers. Their first job was to jump over the Excess
line on the 4-hour chart.

@@b3184 h3
Study 2 (late October 2021)

@@b3185 p
This study starts on the daily chart, read box by box.

@@b3187 p
Figure: Two pushes (A, C) and their corrections (B, D), each boxed.
Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 28 February – 1 March 2022. (The original
chart was GBPUSD, daily; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3188 ul
<li><p><strong>Box A:</strong> buyers engaged with the trend, increasing
their intensity to pull the price up.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Box B:</strong> sellers' intensity was less than buyers'.
They could not attract more sellers to take over the trend.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Box C:</strong> the intensity of volume decreased, but
progress was good. Buyers still had the power to dominate the
price.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Box D:</strong> sellers' intensity increased, and their
progress was better than in B. This puts us on alert.</p></li>

@@b3189 p
So the NetForce showed that buyers had a greater chance to move up
than sellers. If the next SPS cannot move higher than Box C, that would
suggest buyers are losing energy in the struggle. Buyers did show more
intensity in volume at A than in the last D, and the uptrend was still
valid, confirmed by a series of higher highs and higher supports.

@@b3190 p
To check whether the SS at D was at its end point, I zoomed into the
4-hour chart.

@@b3192 p
Figure: After swings inside Excess and Test, a small high at A and
the break of the Test line at B. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 21–24
February 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD, 4-hour, 19–27 October
2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3193 p
Here we had a 6&amp;6 story. At A, buyers' intensity was very low,
and they withdrew quickly. At B, a reversal on increased volume, buyers
could absorb the sellers' force and dominate the trend.

@@b3194 p
If buyers can propagate from this trickery and break the Excess line,
I can consider the EP of the SS on the daily chart confirmed.

@@b3195 h3
Study 3 (late October 2021)

@@b3196 p
The 4-hour chart showed progress for buyers.

@@b3198 p
Figure: Goldilocks: a false break below the Test line to A, then the
return through 50% to B. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 14–16 February 2022.
(The original chart was GBPUSD, 4-hour, 19–28 October 2021; this one
shows the same structure.)

@@b3199 p
At A, sellers decreased their energy to move the price down. In
psychological terms, sellers felt insecure about struggling with buyers,
as the volume at A shows. At B, buyers progressed by a 1-2-3 and met the
sellers' force, as the volume at B shows. Then the market moved between
the Excess and Test lines into the last bar.

@@b3200 p
Look at the volume Propagation principle between the Intersections A
and B:

@@b3201 ul
<li><p>At A, sellers were afraid to engage. Their energy was very
low.</p></li>
<li><p>At B, sellers increased their energy and propagated to struggle
with buyers, right where the 50% occurs.</p></li>

@@b3202 p
Still, I believed sellers' energy was too weak to dominate the trend.
The market was in a bullish condition, and this behaviour by sellers was
only temporary. The 15-minute chart could confirm it, and it told a
wonderful story.

@@b3204 p
Figure: Two tops above the rising line, then the break to A on the
largest volume. Real data: GBPUSD, 30-minute, 22–24 February 2022. (The
original chart was GBPUSD, 15-minute; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b3205 p
It was a bullish Goldilocks, with rising volume at A. That volume
could propagate up to the 4-hour chart and break the smaller Excess line
above 50%. It showed the ability and quality of buyers to absorb all
offers at the 50% level.
