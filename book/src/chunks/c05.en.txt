1.4 Balance

@@b297 p
Balance in the market means looking at both sellers and buyers to
find the remainder factor on the chart. Always keep in mind these
important changes in expectation: the players change their expectation
by being willing to offer or to pay. The same reading also applies to
the Goldilocks. Three cases, each with its selling and buying
mirror:

@@b298 blockquote
<p>1. <strong>Important expectation in the FPS.</strong> Volume rises
during the FPS, showing important selling (or buying), while the SS
shows weak counter-activity with low expectation. Balance: most likely,
sellers are willing to offer at a lower price (or buyers are willing to
pay a higher price).</p>
<p>2. <strong>A regular Jumbo with a change in the FMX.</strong> The
Jumbo has regular volume (no important expectation). The FMX shows a
change in expectation compared with the Jumbo, while the BMX shows
either low expectation from the counter side or high expectation at the
reversal point. Balance: most likely, sellers will offer lower (or
buyers will pay higher).</p>
<p>3. <strong>A regular Jumbo with exhaustion.</strong> The Jumbo has
regular volume. The FMX changes from regular to very low expectation,
and the BMX shows an even lower expectation — exhaustion at the high (or
at the low). Balance: most likely, sellers will offer lower (or buyers
will pay higher).</p>

@@b299 h4
Bernoulli's Axioms

@@b300 blockquote
<p>1. There is no place for conjectures in matters in which one may
reach complete certainty.</p>
<p>2. It is not sufficient to weigh one or another argument. Instead we
must bring together all arguments that we can come to know and that seem
in any way to work toward a proof of the thing.</p>
<p>3. In matters that are uncertain and open to doubt, we should suspend
our actions until we learn more. But if the occasion for action brooks
no delay, then between two actions we should always choose the one that
seems more appropriate, safer, more carefully considered, or more
probable, even if neither action is such in a positive sense.</p>

1.5 Anticipation

@@b302 p
Anticipation is the process that follows the ability to interpret. It
sets the necessary conditions for deciding whether buying or selling is
advisable.

@@b303 p
To anticipate that <strong>buyers are willing to pay higher</strong>,
check:

@@b304 blockquote
<p>1. The current Matrix relates to the larger and the smaller Matrix
(general/partial concept) in a joint relationship.</p>
<p>2. Propagation is in the direction of the larger Matrix.</p>
<p>3. Important buying is confirmed.</p>
<p>4. The 3-C is cancelled.</p>
<p>5. There is an Overlay in the larger Matrix.</p>
<p>6. Expectation (volume) is joint in both Matrices.</p>
<p>7. Profit exceeds risk (the 3-C Setup target).</p>

@@b306 p
Figure: The buying checklist on a chart — joint with the larger
Matrix, Propagation, important buying, 3-C cancel, Propagation Overlay
and joint expectation in volume.

@@b307 p
To anticipate that <strong>sellers are willing to offer
lower</strong>, check the same list in mirror form:

@@b308 blockquote
<p>1. The current Matrix relates to the larger and the smaller Matrix
(general/partial concept) in a joint relationship.</p>
<p>2. Propagation is in the direction of the larger Matrix.</p>
<p>3. Important selling is confirmed.</p>
<p>4. The 3-C is cancelled.</p>
<p>5. There is an Overlay in the larger Matrix.</p>
<p>6. Expectation (volume) is joint in both Matrices.</p>
<p>7. Profit exceeds risk (the 3-C Setup target).</p>

@@b310 p
Figure: The selling checklist on a chart — the mirror of the buying
case, with important selling and a Propagation Overlay in the larger
Matrix.

1.6 Managing the Trade

@@b312 p
The biggest problem traders face is taking a loss. When we deal with
chance, a loss or a win is just a chance. It takes a habit to stand firm
in front of a series of small losses. Do not hurry to increase the lot
size after a loss in the hope of profit. That action can destroy a
trading account immediately.

@@b313 p
The secret of a winning attitude:

@@b314 ul
<li><p><strong>After a series of losses</strong>: lower your expectation
by decreasing the lot size, and review your feedback.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>After a series of wins</strong>: raise your expectation
by increasing the lot, slowly.</p></li>

@@b315 p
Professional traders use this attitude, and we can see it easily on
the chart. When buyers take profit from their expectation by riding the
FPS, they lower their expectation in the SS. Then they repeat: they
enter again at the EP of the SS, expecting to increase their profit.

@@b317 p
Figure: Buyers ride the FPS with high expectation (volume), lower it
during the SS, then enter again at the EP of the SS.

@@b318 p
The best way to apply Goodman's theory is through experiment and
investigation, and then to profit with confidence.

@@b319 blockquote
<p>"Chance, as we understand it, supposes the Existence of things, and
their general known Properties: that a number of Dice, for instance,
being thrown, each of them shall settle upon one or other of its Bases.
After which, the Probability of an assigned Chance, that is of some
particular disposition of the Dice, becomes as proper a subject of
Investigation as any other quantity or Ratio can be."</p>

The Visionary
Trader: Connecting the Dots (2024)

@@b321 p
In this short paper I describe one habit of mind I call being a
visionary trader. I explain the two steps behind it, and then I show it
on a real gold trade, from the daily Matrix Overlay down to a 5-minute
entry.

@@b322 h3
Know Yourself First

@@b323 p
Every trader comes to recognize his emotional patterns, the good and
the bad. He learns what he needs to fix to trade better. A feedback loop
tells him, in detail, about his own process.

@@b324 p
You do not need to be perfect. No one is perfect. You improve
gradually from your results. A deep understanding of "know yourself" is
the key to consistent success, and it is inside you.

@@b325 h3
The Visionary Habit: Two
Steps

@@b326 p
One important and highly emotionally intelligent skill is to be a
visionary. It has two steps:

@@b327 blockquote
<p>1. <strong>Collect facts from everywhere</strong> until they become
very strong evidence that points to a way of winning.</p>
<p>2. <strong>Look for new facts</strong> that come to you and support
that conclusion.</p>

@@b328 p
What do I see now? What do I want to see next? The two questions are
connected and fit together, like drawing a line through many dots.
Connect the dots! First you draw the line; then you watch forward for a
new dot, and draw it again.

@@b330 p
Figure: The visionary habit as a loop — see now, expect next, then
redraw the line with each new dot.

The Concept on a Chart

@@b332 p
Let me illustrate the concept on a chart. Suppose you are looking for
a 1-hour Setup, and you use the daily chart as the Outward chart to
analyze the Propagation.

@@b334 p
Figure: Price returns to the EP line at BP Matrix 1 and pushes to a
new high at the EP of Matrix 2. Real data: XAUUSD (gold), 1-hour, 28
February – 4 March 2022. (The original chart was Gold daily,
January–July 2024; this one shows the same structure on gold, on the
1-hour chart of the window the data covers.)

@@b335 p
Here we have a Matrix Overlay. The logic behind it: the sellers who
held orders saw all their profit erased at the EP of Matrix 2. At the
same time, this level attracts selling orders, because it is a new high
(on my original gold chart, the highest price for gold ever). One can
examine the feeling of the crowd when prices rise into a new high. The
best strategy is to sell a new high and buy a new low!

@@b336 p
In my opinion the Matrix Overlay works very well when its components
are Complex-Complex. A complex in the MO tells us about the strength of
the trend, especially if there is a count. I look for complexes as a
change in direction and in volatility. I cannot go on without explaining
this behavior, pointing from the higher high to the lower low.

@@b338 p
Figure: The first complex after BP Matrix 1, a downward 1-2-3 whose
wave 3 makes a new low below 1. Real data: AUDUSD, 30-minute, 28 Feb – 2
Mar 2022. (The original chart was Gold daily, March–July 2024; gold is
now in the data, but in the gold window available (14 February – 4 March
2022) wave 3 does not make a new low below 1, so this one still shows
the structure on AUDUSD.)

@@b339 h4
Matrix 1: The Energy of
Selling

@@b340 p
In the first Matrix we need to measure the actual selling or buying
impact on the Setup. The 1-2-3 tells us about the energy of selling. It
is the point where selling energy grows: the EP of wave 3 is a new low
below 1.

@@b341 p
If supply is high and demand is low, price will drop under its own
weight. It is a tug of war.

@@b342 h4
Matrix 2: The Ultimate Test

@@b343 p
In Matrix 2 the buyers have something to say: a 1-2-3 with a new
high. This is the ultimate test of the selling energy. Either the
sellers renew their energy and drop the price, or buying drives the
price much higher.

@@b344 p
Most probably the buyers are losing their energy. The crowd believes
"price is too high, better to wait for the next correction". With this
belief, demand is very low and supply is high. So it is time to spot a
Setup at this location.

Figure: The complete Complex-Complex Matrix Overlay, with the 1-2-3
of Matrix 1 down and the 1-2-3 of Matrix 2 up to the new high. Real
data: XAUUSD (gold), 30-minute, 1–4 March 2022. (The original chart was
Gold daily, March–July 2024; this one shows the same structure on gold,
in the window the data covers. Here wave 3 of Matrix 1 holds just above
1.)
