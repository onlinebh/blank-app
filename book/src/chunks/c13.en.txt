@@b846 h3
The King: a picture of
harmonic culture

@@b847 p
To make this easier to see, imagine that this harmonic culture rules
like a King. The King does not create price. He only manages it.

@@b848 ul
<li><p>When the price of goods is very low, sellers want to push it
higher and higher. The King knows that buyers could pay more, but only
by falling into poverty. So he stops the sellers' greed and sets the
price as a floor.</p></li>
<li><p>When the price is already very high, the King judges what his
people can afford. If buyers can pay with abundance and without poverty,
he allows sellers to raise the price further.</p></li>

@@b849 p
This explains two things that confuse many traders:

@@b850 blockquote
<p>1. Price can make a new high even with fewer buyers, because the King
judges that buyers can still pay with abundance.</p>
<p>2. Price can make a new low even with fewer sellers, because the King
judges that paying a higher price would bring buyers into poverty, even
if they could technically pay.</p>

@@b851 h4
How the King reverses a
trend

@@b852 ul
<li><p>When the King judges that buyers can now pay a higher price with
abundance, he changes his judgment. He allows sellers to sell to a new
high. This is a reversal from down to up.</p></li>
<li><p>When buyers show poverty at the current price and cannot pay with
abundance, the King judges that sellers must move down. This is a
reversal from up to down.</p></li>

@@b853 h4
The King judges by
experiment

@@b854 p
The King learns his people's ability by experiment. First he tests
how buyers react when they are in poverty; then his judgment refuses to
change the price. A later experiment shows buyers who can pay with
abundance; then his judgment agrees to raise the price, based on that
last experiment.

@@b855 p
So there is a King who manages price. He only manages it.

@@b856 p
<em>Note: "King" is only a descriptive word for the harmonic culture
belief, the power of culture belief.</em>

@@b858 p
Figure: The King judges by experiment: each test of buyers at a price
leads to an inference and to the King's justice. Abundance allows a
higher price; poverty forces it lower.

@@b872 h3
Smaller 1-2-3s
are the blood of the larger 1-2-3

@@b873 p
Every justice that comes from an experiment supports one of two
things: the larger FPS or SPS, or the larger SS. It is always a 1-2-3
inside a larger 1-2-3.

@@b874 ul
<li><p>A smaller 1-2-3 either supports the larger one or stops
supporting it. When the smaller 1-2-3 stops supporting the larger, the
next move is the SS.</p></li>
<li><p>A larger 1-2-3 without support from smaller 1-2-3s will move in
the opposite direction.</p></li>
<li><p>The smaller 1-2-3 plays as blood to the larger 1-2-3.</p></li>

@@b875 p
When justice works to support a larger 1-2-3 (a larger timeframe, in
real time), it means fresh blood is flowing in a new direction.

@@b877 p
Figure: Smaller 1-2-3s are the blood of the larger 1-2-3: while they
support it, the FPS or SPS continues; when they stop, the SS comes.

@@b886 h2
Market Circulation (2021)

@@b887 p
Every day has 24 hours. A week has 7 days, a month about 4 weeks, a
year 12 months and 4 seasons. Life moves in cycles, and so does the
market. In this study I want to show how much a trader can gain by
reading the cycle in the market. I define that cycle simply: a swing
from extreme happiness to extreme sadness, and back again.

@@b888 h3
Part 1: Cycles, energy
and the Crosspoint

@@b889 h3
Projectiles and potential
energy

@@b890 p
In physics we study projectile motion. The greater the velocity, the
further the projectile travels, just like directional movement in price.
Each projectile has a different velocity.

@@b891 p
Here is an example from the market.

@@b893 p
Figure: Each leg is a projectile with its own angle, and the circled
lows are where the market stored energy before the next launch. Real
data: EURGBP, 1-hour, 14–28 February 2022. (The original chart was
EURGBP daily, March to October 2019; this one shows the same structure
on the same symbol, in the window the data covers.)

@@b894 p
Each projectile, or vector, has a different angle, and the angle
shows its strength. The market can only go up or down. Everything else
is the market in a "hash", charging its battery. In physics this is
potential energy.

@@b895 p
When the market sits in a time hash, it is only building potential
energy for the next move, down or up. That proves what Goodman says.
When we deal only with down and up, the market looks very simple, and it
really is simple.

@@b896 h4
Where to board the bus

@@b897 p
I have spent a lot of time thinking deeply about all the Goodman
setups. Where is the safest point to board the bus? It is easy: where
the bus is in potential energy, at the top of the hill before it rolls.
That is how the 6&amp;6 and the Goldilocks work. Each of these
potential-energy states leads either up or down.

@@b899 p
Figure: A down leg, then a box of potential energy where a 6&amp;6
forms, then the up leg that releases it. Real data: EURGBP, 1-hour,
16–25 February 2022. (The original chart was EURGBP daily, February 2014
to May 2015; this one shows the same structure on the same symbol, in
the window the data covers.)

@@b900 p
In Goodman we call this a station point: the place where a down trend
or an up trend reduces its speed until its velocity reaches zero and it
stops, like a car at a police checkpoint.

@@b901 p
In brief, we use the Overlay to find a station point that can turn
the current trend, shown by a 6&amp;6 or a Goldilocks. From this we know
what we want to see. That is the most important question:

@@b902 blockquote
<p>What do I want to see next?</p>

@@b903 p
Without it, we trade without seeing anything. There is no real
purpose; it is random.
