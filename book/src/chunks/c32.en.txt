@@b2098 h3
Price Makes Volume

@@b2099 p
The market is a voting machine. New buyers come when price rises, and
new sellers come when price declines. Price makes volume.

@@b2100 p
Once price rises above the last top, volume may increase. That shows
new buyers are joining the move. As more new buyers arrive, the move
goes much higher, because the previous sellers withdraw from the market
and become interested in the new move.

@@b2101 h3
Three Steps to Qualify a
Trade

@@b2102 blockquote
<p>1. <strong>Start with the 3-C cancel.</strong> Use it as the
reference point for studying volume.</p>
<p>2. <strong>Look for a Matrix Overlay at the same point.</strong></p>
<p>3. <strong>Do the Inward analysis.</strong> Watch for significant
changes in volume. A significant price level makes volume. Look for
volume increasing on the upswing and decreasing on the downswing that
makes a higher low.</p>

@@b2111 h4
Putting it together

@@b2112 p
Volume can only increase or decrease, and both tell us something. If
volume decreases while price makes a higher low, it means a lack of
sellers. Price fails to attract sellers at the 3-C cancel and also at
the BP of the Matrix Overlay. Putting all of these together makes the
picture clearer.

@@b2122 h4
The key

@@b2123 p
Compare the selling with what the Dagger line produced. Selling
increased at 7. Then sellers dominated the trend on lower volume, as at
14 and 16, and were able to push the downswing much further. But at 24,
sellers did not want to increase their flow to protect the line. No
increase in volume, no lower low, no further downswing. This is the
key.

@@b2124 h2
Volume Today: When
Everyone Agrees (2025–2026)

@@b2125 p
My first two papers on volume asked a narrow question: does volume
confirm the trade I already see? In 2021 I read volume against the
matrix. In 2023 I used it to qualify a trade in three steps. Today I ask
a different question: <strong>how many people agree right now?</strong>
When almost everyone agrees, the move is finished. Volume consensus is
exhaustion. If everyone is selling, it means buy. If everyone is buying,
it means sell.

@@b2126 h3
From confirmation to
consensus

@@b2127 p
The change did not come from volume alone. It came from years of
reading sentiment beside it. In September 2024 I wrote to Michael what
my own testing had shown:

@@b2128 blockquote
<p>"Anyway my back testing shows the best Sentiment indicators are
Bullish consensus, second OI, third Volume. What i find when BC above
90. This reflects in Volume, because everyone is buying at that price
level. (There is a willing to let money on the table) every one bet.
This is a real sign of turning point. But in my test, many false signals
from volume if not confirmed with BC." (26 September 2024)</p>

@@b2129 p
So volume was third in the ranking, and alone it gave false signals.
What made it useful was the same thing that makes Bullish Consensus
useful: the extreme. A reading of 90 means the crowd has already placed
its bet. A volume extreme means the same thing, measured in ticks
instead of opinions.

@@b2130 p
In the same letter I explained what open interest adds:

@@b2131 blockquote
<p>"Price at 100, but OI increase it shows new buying (willing). Same
Price at 100, but OI Decrase, shows buying out of the market." (26
September 2024)</p>

@@b2132 p
Two weeks earlier I had watched silver change character exactly this
way:

@@b2133 blockquote
<p>"As I anticipated, the market changed in volatility from Aggressive
Selling into a lack of selling a kind of consensus." (9 September
2024)</p>

@@b2134 p
Aggressive selling first, then nobody left to sell. That is the shape
of a consensus extreme: the crowd agrees, acts all at once, and then has
nothing left.

@@b2135 h3
Why raw forex volume
cannot be trusted

@@b2136 p
Most of my trading is forex, and forex has no central exchange. The
volume your platform shows is tick volume: the number of price changes
on one broker's feed. It is not the number of contracts traded. I said
it plainly in November 2025:

@@b2137 blockquote
<p>"Most of the volume in the market. It's artificial volume. Type of
scam. Just money rounds and rounds. I think the cumulative volume. Its a
good idea. Volume by time losing thier value." (21 November 2025)</p>

@@b2138 p
Two ideas sit in that note. First, one bar of volume means little;
what counts is volume <strong>accumulated</strong> along a move. Second,
volume measured by the clock loses value; the move should decide where a
count starts and stops, not the hour.

December 2025). I had tried to program it in Pine Script years before,
using open interest. The next day I wrote:

@@b2140 blockquote
<p>"The descartes raito. I did some to let it work on forex. Tick volume
mixed with ATR. I think there is relationship between voltiltiy and

@@b2141 p
This is the same problem again. Forex has no open interest and no
true volume, so every tool built for futures must be adapted.
