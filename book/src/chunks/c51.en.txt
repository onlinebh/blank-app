Study 4 (late October 2021)

@@b3207 p
Here I read cycles across two timeframes.

@@b3209 p
Figure: A completed bullish cycle, an incomplete push that breeds a
bearish cycle, and a new bullish cycle. Real data: GBPUSD, 4-hour, 14
February – 2 March 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD, daily, March
2020 – October 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3210 p
On the daily chart, A was a complete 1-2-3. At X, the cross point met
selling pressure; compare all the blue arrows with X. At B, we had an
incomplete 1-2-3. Buyers could not absorb sellers in the A area, so this
was buyers' terminal point.

@@b3211 p
At C, sellers entered and dominated the trend, but with very little
Nash. At Y, see how the cross point showed buyers' energy inside the
bearish cycle. At D, I expected the bullish cycle to begin in the coming
weeks.

@@b3213 p
Figure: The range at the top of the rise: bullish cycle complete,
bearish cycle complete, new bullish cycle. Real data: GBPUSD, 30-minute,
15–18 February 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD, 4-hour, 16
September – 29 October 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3214 p
The cycles become very clear when two timeframes are combined: each
smaller cycle follows the larger daily cycle and confirms it. On the
4-hour chart the bearish cycle was complete, and new energy appeared in
volume at A. In the reaction, volume decreased. For the first time
sellers were losing energy, and this generated a new cycle in the
direction of the larger daily cycle.

@@b3215 p
This is a type of Overlay combination, one that shows the maximum and
the minimum.

@@b3216 h3
Study 5 (early November 2021)

@@b3217 p
In the last report I anticipated the market would move up from D. But
it is part of the market's adventure that buyers or sellers can withdraw
at any time, and that reverses the trend. The market is a wonderful
place that always surprises you. Otherwise trading would be a boring
job.

@@b3219 p
Figure: Turning points A–G matched to their volume bars. Real data:
GBPUSD, 15-minute, 16–18 February 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD;
this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3220 blockquote
<p>1. <strong>A:</strong> the highest volume in the FPS. Buyers
increased their energy by absorbing all offers.</p>
<p>2. <strong>B:</strong> in the SS, sellers could not carry the market
to a lower low, as the decreasing volume shows. When the market cannot
move lower, it moves up to attract more people.</p>
<p>3. <strong>C:</strong> the SPS had great momentum and an excellent
End Point. For a while everything looked bullish.</p>
<p>4. <strong>D:</strong> volume decreased, which confirmed the bullish
inference.</p>
<p>5. <strong>E:</strong> the danger sign. Buyers did not increase their
energy to make a higher high. Compare E with A and C.</p>
<p>6. <strong>F:</strong> volume increased as the price fell, with the
ability to create a 1-2-3 down. This was the first real show of sellers'
intensity, compared with B and D.</p>
<p>7. <strong>G:</strong> the Return Zone, where we go short.</p>

@@b3221 p
Volume can be very ambiguous at F and G, since both stand at the same
level. But look left. F came after E, where buyers had no energy, and it
was the highest energy compared with B and D. G sat at the Return Zone,
and compared with A and C, we can assume buyers at G were weak.

@@b3222 p
Even so, I expected the bullish cycle to continue after this
temporary reaction, because the Overlay combination on the daily chart
was still active, unless the market showed major symptoms.

Study 5–6 (early November
2021)

@@b3224 p
When you want to judge the market condition, you must practice a lot
of patience, and a lot of thanks.

@@b3226 p
Figure: An incomplete cycle: I, II, III and a shallow IV before the
breakout. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 23–25 February 2022. (The original
chart was GBPUSD; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3227 p
Angle Propagation developed from A to B and then to C, with an
incomplete cycle. It shows how buyers struggled to dominate the trend,
pulling the market at A, B and C while sellers could not react properly.
It is wonderful to track buyers' attacks:

@@b3228 ul
<li><p><strong>I:</strong> the first attack on sellers. It could not
stop the selling flow. The angle from A to B showed some improvement,
but not enough.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>II:</strong> buyers absorbed all offers like a
sponge.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>III:</strong> buyers moved on very low volume. This was
volume noise; it did not reveal buyers' true energy.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>IV:</strong> buyers again stopped the movement on
increasing volume, with an incomplete cycle. Here volume gave a better
message about rising buying energy.</p></li>

@@b3229 p
Angle Propagation C showed a great improvement. In each down move,
buyers improved their quality in absorbing offers. The last few bars
broke the green line, the Excess line, which was the first sign that
buyers could develop this into a 1-2-3.

@@b3230 p
When the market wants to move into an uptrend, everything is usually
in doubt. But as I showed, this tactic can track buyers' attack to
dominate the trend clearly and with a high degree of certainty.

Study 7 (November 2021)

@@b3232 p
After buyers propagated into a 1-2-3, the market retreated to the
Return Zone.

@@b3234 p
Figure: The price breaks above the box and then returns to it. Real

@@b3236 h3
Study 8 (early November 2021)

@@b3237 p
In this short report I show how to judge the strength or weakness of
the market, and how to profit from the change from one to the other. It
takes only four easy steps:

@@b3238 blockquote
<p>1. Filter price action into oscillator waves.</p>
<p>2. Draw Propagation lines to read the market from the sense of
Propagation.</p>
<p>3. Read the Propagation lines as buyers' and sellers' mental energy,
and judge who has the greater energy.</p>
<p>4. Make money from that judgement, as an investment policy.</p>

@@b3240 p
Figure: The whole trade: Long, test1–test2 with Stops, Cover Long at
test3, Short at test4, test5. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 14–24 February
2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD, 4-hour, 23 September – 5 November
2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3241 h4
Steps 2 and 3: a bullish
indication

@@b3242 p
The black line represents the 1-2-3, and we watch the Propagation
reaction at Test 2. Buyers' energy re-entered at Test 2 after a period
of low volume. The Propagation ran from 29 September to 8 October with
continuous buying, and Test 2 took a very short time.

@@b3244 p
Figure: Long, then test1 and test2 with the Stop raised under each.
Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 14–17 February 2022. (The original chart was
GBPUSD, 4-hour, 28 September – 13 October 2021; this one shows the same

@@b3245 h4
The turn: a bearish
indication

@@b3246 p
The market extended buyers' ability into a larger 1-2-3. Then the 3-C
showed buyers were exhausted, and the 3-C was cancelled. The correction
was small, 20% of the Propagation, suggesting buyers wanted to keep
their gains close to the top and sell to new buyers who buy high after
such a move.

@@b3247 p
For the first time we had a bearish Goldilocks sign. After an
increase in volume the market was weak, especially in the last rally
into Test 3 on decreasing volume. The market also sat in congestion from
20 October 12:00 to 26 October, a long sideways move with no progress
for buyers. Logically, all long positions had to be covered and reversed
to short (Step 4).

@@b3249 p
Figure: Cancel of the upward cycle and Cover Long at test3. Real
data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 14–21 February 2022. (The original chart was
GBPUSD, 4-hour, 29 September – 26 October 2021; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b3250 p
The symptoms of buyers losing energy were very clear, while sellers'
energy was rising. The abnormal volume suggested old buyers selling to
new buyers while new sellers entered. That imbalance creates a bearish
trend, and a great opportunity to go short.

@@b3251 h4
Bearish domination

@@b3252 p
The market is like a movie. The red line shows the first 1-2-3 down.
At Test 4, despite all that volume, the rally was very short in time.
The faster and steeper a rally, the quicker it dies compared with the
earlier rallies, and within hours volume decreased.

@@b3253 p
If you did not go short at Test 3, you could still make money at Test
4 as a Goodman Setup, with the stop at least at the top (Step 4).
