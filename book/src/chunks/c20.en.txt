@@b1284 ul

showing a healthy downtrend, is box D, then box C with the lowest gains,
at 50% of its SS. Just look at the FPS volume, the SPS volume and their
ranges.

@@b1286 p
So the healthiest to trade are box D, then box C. On A and B I am
neutral, because there is still a chance for them to move up.

@@b1304 h3
A
live study: the minds of buyers and sellers on H1 (first studied on
NZDUSD)

@@b1305 p
In this study I look at the relationship between human desire and
action: belief and the fight. I use the Goodman Box to mark the border
of the fight between buyers and sellers. Volume shows how much liquidity
each side has to support its fight. Time shows whether the fight runs
quickly or very slowly, and either can kill buyers or sellers. In the
end price action tells us honestly what happens inside the war.

@@b1306 p
Let us start: H1, first studied on NZDUSD and shown here on real
GBPUSD data.

@@b1308 p
Figure: The chart with no drawing: the conflict cannot be read yet.
Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 15 to 25 February 2022. (The original chart
was NZDUSD 1-hour, August 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b1309 p
Without any drawing, no one can read the conflict in this chart. That
is why we must connect with the market and touch the sellers' mind and
the buyers' mind. Then we decide which side has more intensity and is
eager to take the flag of domination. Both sides want to be king of the
kingdom.

@@b1310 p
I start from the top of the chart and go down, following the market.
I do not look at anything on the higher timeframe. Let us read those
minds.

@@b1311 h4
First box: the oasis is an
illusion

@@b1313 p
Figure: The first box: buyers ride price upward and everything looks
healthy. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 15 to 25 February 2022. (The
original chart was NZDUSD 1-hour, August 2021; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b1314 p
Buyers ride the horse upward and everything looks healthy. But what
we see on the chart is noise. This move is not as promising as it looks.
It looks as if buyers are healthy and one can buy. This oasis is just an
illusion, part of the noise. The volume tells the story behind this
upswing.

@@b1316 p
Figure: The first box with numbered volume points 1 to 5; the volume
at the top of the box does not fit the size of its bars. Real data:
GBPUSD, 1-hour, 15 to 25 February 2022. (The original chart was NZDUSD
1-hour, August 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b1317 blockquote
<p>1. Point 1 is the beginning point of the box.</p>
<p>2. At 2 buyers begin to control the land; then for several hours the
market moves sideways.</p>
<p>3. At 3 volume rises, but relative to the size of the bar it is fine;
no problem.</p>
<p>4. At 4 the size of the bar does not fit the volume, at the end of
the bar.</p>
<p>5. A few hours later, at 5, the market moves down on falling
volume.</p>

@@b1318 p
Why does such volume appear at the top of the box? New traders,
sellers, have arrived from different levels of the market. My question
is: does this new army add to the sellers' power or to the buyers'?
Which side does it want to help?

@@b1319 p
As a guideline: when volume spikes at the end of a box, most of the
time the new army wants to help the opposite force. At 5 the market
moves down by itself, like a gun already loaded with a bullet, the
volume. Volume helps us see that this box holds a new army, so be
cautious.

@@b1320 h4
The new seller army and
the red mark

@@b1322 p
Figure: "Alert: new seller army" on the first box; the next box shows

@@b1323 p
The new box shows sellers taking the market after the new seller army
at 3 and 4. At 6, again at the end of a box, there is an announcement: a
new buyer army wants to fight the sellers who came at 3.

@@b1324 p
I put a red mark above the first box. It tests the army's power, its
desire. If the buyers are truly strong, they must move up to that red
level to meet the 1-2-3 requirement. At 7, after the new buyer army, the
market stays silent for a while. The next box is the time to fight. We
will watch which army dies first at the apex of the battle.

@@b1325 h4
The sellers' army camp

@@b1327 p
Figure: A sharp up box shows eager buyers, but sellers enter in force
at 8 and 9. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 15 to 25 February 2022. (The
original chart was NZDUSD 1-hour, August 2021; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b1328 p
The new box, with its sharp angle, shows buyers eager to control the
kingdom. But the sellers' army has another opinion: at 9 they enter in
force to stop the buyers' progress.

@@b1329 p
Look at 8: volume and bar are relatively good. At 9 the bar is small.
That confirms our reading: buyers cannot move quickly because of the new
seller army at 8 and 9. It may also confirm that the buyers die before
completing the 1-2-3 cycle. So from 1 to 9 I call it the sellers' army
camp. Notice how volume rises at the top of each box.

@@b1331 p
Figure: "Sellers' army force camp": at A new buyers enter near the
apex, and at B buyers rise without volume. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 15
to 25 February 2022. (The original chart was NZDUSD 1-hour, August 2021;
this one shows the same structure.)

@@b1332 p
In the new box the chart moves to the apex. At A, new traders think
the market will go up. These new traders are bad players, as B confirms.
On the up move at B, after the buyers have failed many times before,
there is no rise in volume.

@@b1333 p
The message: buyers are losing their power. They cannot fight; they
are old in the battle.

@@b1334 h4
The tiny box: go short

@@b1336 p

@@b1337 p
This box is tiny, and it is a sell signal. A tiny box shows buyers

@@b1338 p
Think about it. Sellers controlled the market against many buyers at
3, 5, 8 and A. Here it is a piece of cake for sellers to push the market
down. That is why the box is tiny. When volume is very small and the
range is small too, that is the confirmation.

@@b1339 p
The message: buyers are very weak. Go short.

@@b1345 h4
The continuation box

@@b1347 p
Figure: A continuation box with the TP marked: the bounce at E stops
under the TP. Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 15 to 25 February 2022. (The
original chart was NZDUSD 1-hour, August 2021; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b1348 p
This box acts as a continuation box. There is always some volatility
inside a trend, such as E, which closes inside the box and can confuse
traders about the down move. But with the box we are safe. Look how the
buyers stop under the TP.

@@b1349 p
Volume at C was very, very low, showing that buyers had no interest
in the move at E. The volume at C relative to the size of bar C is good.
So it is confirmed: buyers are too weak to support the market. We move
our stop order to around the TP level. Always protect your gain and kill
your loss.

@@b1351 p
Figure: The next down box: a volume spike at F mid-box, and more

@@b1352 p
At F there is a volume spike, but no worry; it is in the middle of
the box. At G volume rises again, and the close of the last bar shows
some strength. It suggests the market is preparing for the next rally.
This angle is slower than the last down box. Still, there is no sign of
an uptrend yet; the crown is still with the sellers' force.

@@b1353 h4
New buyers arrive

@@b1355 p
Figure: "Alert": at H new traders enter, with volume rising on the up

@@b1356 p
At H new traders enter the market as a contingency. The amount of
buying rises persistently with every up bar. We have not seen that habit
since the downtrend began at the apex. So here we move the stop order
above that rally, and we prepare for an uptrend.

@@b1358 p
Figure: The slowest down box, with no gain and lower volume at J.
Real data: GBPUSD, 1-hour, 15 to 25 February 2022. (The original chart
was NZDUSD 1-hour, August 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b1359 p
The angle of this box is the slowest of all, with no gain. It
suggests the sellers are losing their force. For the first time, volume
at J is falling: no new sellers at this level. At H we had many new
buyers. Here we move the stop order.

@@b1360 p
The message: a new buyer army has arrived, while the sellers give up
or move away. Prepare for an uptrend.
