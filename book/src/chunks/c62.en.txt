Where I Stand Today

@@b3687 p
This book climbed a ladder, from being to knowing, seeing, deciding,
acting, measuring, doing right, feeling and learning. Before my last
letter to Michael, I want to say plainly how I trade now, in 2026. Not
how I wish I traded. How I do it.

@@b3688 p
<strong>A small family of charts.</strong> I do not watch the whole
market. I keep a short list and let it change slowly: EURCAD, GBPAUD,
EURAUD, AUDCAD, NZDCAD, gold, silver, sometimes the Nasdaq or Bitcoin.
On 2026-06-06 I wrote: "today i added two charts to my watchlist . I
picked one . neglect another ." That is the whole job of a watchlist. It
is for saying no.

@@b3689 p
<strong>From the most general to the most specific.</strong> I start
Outward, on the chart where the Propagation is, and move inward only if
something there interests me. On 2026-07-11 I shorted EURAUD because "i
see it more than 3 propgation in daily chart. Oh its time to short". The
daily told me the story was old. The smaller charts told me when.

@@b3690 p
<strong>Two speeds.</strong> For quick trades I use FireStarter, on
15-minute charts inside the 4-hour picture. On 2026-06-10 I wrote: "I
trade Firestarter in the morning here." On 2026-08-31 I gave Michael
three reasons I value it: many trades a month, "high frequency less
emotional", and a high win rate with good risk/reward. For the larger
trades I still wait for the full setup, a Molokai or a PTS, with an
Outward overlay. On 2026-09-08 I passed on EURCAD because I was "waiting
for a nest , the bar it's risky. Bad money management", and on a Bitcoin
66 because it had "nice setup but no overlay".

@@b3691 p
<strong>Size is decided before the chart.</strong> I still decide my
maximum drawdown first and divide it across thirty trades. Every trade
spends one soul. In FireStarter the size never changes, in losses or in
profits. In my other trading, the Golden Ratios every ten trades tell me
whether to keep the size or lower it.

@@b3692 p
<strong>The high-percentage duck.</strong> On 2026-07-11 I wrote:
"The key for long-term success is to find high percentage duck trade."
And the aim inside each trade is still breakeven first. When the stop is
at breakeven, I can let the market take its time.

@@b3693 p
<strong>Volume as a witness.</strong> I read volume beside price, but
only volume that persists. My own indicator, Continuity + Consensus
Extreme, lets volume build only while the same side keeps winning, bar
after bar, and marks the record peak just before the streak breaks. I
described it to Michael on 2026-09-04: "No ratios, no smoothing, no
cumulative-forever sums — just raw volume, gated by whether the same
side kept winning bar after bar." I also try other views of the same
truth. On 2026-09-26 I wrote that I had programmed a Pugh chart on
TradingView: "less noise".

@@b3694 p
<strong>Discipline built into the tools.</strong> I do not trust my
willpower on a bad day. In May 2026 I programmed a trading journal with
an automatic alert "if less than golden ratio , or BIG size lot". In
September I finished the Poka-Yoke expert, with its twelve layers. The
tools hold the rules so that my mind can stay on the chart.

@@b3695 p
<strong>Being proactive.</strong> Charlie's question is still my
first question: "What do I see now and what do I want to see next!" On
2026-04-28 I wrote back: "Proactive actually affects my life
positively." Before a session I mark the charts. During it, I wait for
what I said I wanted to see.

@@b3696 p
<strong>What I still study.</strong> On 2026-08-13 I wrote:
"Sometimes I make several notes: probability, combinations and the
chart!. Even if it fails, it gives a hint." I still study Market
Environments, the relation between volatility and volume, the logic of
abduction, and the Codex. Every course I build teaches me again. "I
learn from these courses!! a lot !!!!!" I wrote on 2026-08-22.

@@b3697 p
On 2026-07-11 I told Michael: "Actually i enjoy goodman. Now become
my second nature." That is where I stand. Fifty years in, the method is
no longer something I apply. It is the way I look. Everything is a
1-2-3.
