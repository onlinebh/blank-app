##CTX Part: Rung 4 · Deciding: Goodman's Tools and the Setup | Section: Overlay Combination (2021)

@@b1744 h3
Knowledge versus wisdom

@@b1745 p
What is the difference between what we know and what we learn?
Knowledge can be found everywhere. Fitting that knowledge to serve our
aims and purpose is a pearl of wisdom.

@@b1746 p
A campaign in trading means looking deeply at every Goodman point,
using the Goodman process, to output one of three decisions: Long, Short
or Don't Trade.

@@b1747 h4
Anatomy of a campaign

@@b1748 p
Instead of thinking of one trade, think of 10 trades as one campaign.
No one can rely on one trade.

@@b1749 blockquote
<p>1. <strong>Operation.</strong> Always look out for trades (Tracking),
to find candidates for the 10-trade campaign.</p>
<p>2. <strong>Strategy.</strong> Before entering, make sure there is a
logical ability that can make the setup go (Staging): identify the
Overlays for a possible trade.</p>
<p>3. <strong>Technique.</strong> The trader's skills after deciding to
enter: where to put the stop-loss, when to raise the stop order, when to
take profit.</p>
<p>4. <strong>Feedback.</strong> Was the campaign successful: Y &lt; X,
Y &gt; X or Y = X? What was the most important element that made it
succeed or fail? The more questions, the better. Learn from losses and
wins.</p>

@@b1751 p
Figure: The four stages of a ten-trade campaign and its three
possible decisions.

@@b1752 p
In brief, each campaign has two things: buy now and sell later. When
you sell later, the result is Y &lt; X, Y &gt; X or Y = X.

@@b1753 p
On the next pages I show 5 trades as part of a campaign, and also
some trades I avoided because there was not enough Overlay. Each uses a
1-hour Setup with a 1-day Overlay. Remember, the aim of every campaign
is 10, 4, 1.

@@b1757 p
Is this trade Y &gt; X, Y &lt; X or Y = X, and why? What do I see
now? I want to read the real force: which is greater, buyers or
sellers?

@@b1759 p
Figure: The Jumbo box A with a volume surge at its end, then the
small box B, where late sellers have no future. Real data: AUDUSD,
30-minute, 23–25 Feb 2022. (The original chart was TRYJPY 1-hour; this
one shows the same structure.)

@@b1760 p
We have two boxes. The range A is a Jumbo. But look at the end: the
volume increased abnormally, meaning many transactions took place there.
What is the future for that amount of sales?

@@b1761 p
Remember: at the end of any deal, the future shows who is the loser.
Losers are those who buy and the price moves down, and those who sell
and the price moves up. In both cases Y &lt; X.

@@b1762 p
At B there is no future for those who sold the market late. The
market is now dominated by buyers; the reward is with buyers. Can you
see there is no reward with sellers at B? In the middle of box B the
volume increased, but the future belonged to buyers. Look at the range
of B: do you want that kind of profit?

@@b1763 p
The lesson: be cautious when volume increases at the end of a box and
the next box is a small range.

@@b1766 p
Is the next one a 3? And where is the possible reward?

Where is the problem? Instead of going up to 3, it moved down to B.

@@b1770 p
Let us go to the higher time frame (the daily in my original).

@@b1772 p
Figure: After 1 and 2, price stores energy between the excess line
and the test line, near the low of 2. Real data: NZDUSD, 1-hour, 15 Feb
– 1 Mar 2022. (The original chart was TRYJPY daily; this one shows the

@@b1773 p
What do I see now? The current price is between the test and the
excess lines, storing energy, near the bottom of Matrix Overlay point 2.
Let us judge the market:

@@b1774 blockquote
<p>1. The market moves between the excess and test lines, storing energy
for a new move.</p>
<p>2. The market is very near the bottom at 2.</p>
<p>3. We have a Jumbo with a PTS on the 1-hour.</p>
<p>4. It is newly bullish on the daily (very near the bottom) and on the
1-hour.</p>
<p>5. We still do not know when the market can break out of the excess
line.</p>
<p>6. The market is non-trending on the daily, but the 1-hour shows an
uptrend.</p>
<p>7. There is still no 1-2-3 on the daily chart.</p>
<p>8. The market environment (ME) shows very low directionality and very
low volatility.</p>

@@b1775 p
The problem is: can it break out of the excess line, or will it move
sideways? This trade is Y = X, 5 of 10 risky, because the daily is
non-trending and storing energy. Looking at buyers, though, they can
stop the downward movement on the daily, so it is still bullish. If
someone pushed me to enter, I would enter a buy only.

@@b1776 p
But one must enter only the best trades. This one is risky for me, so
I will pass. I do not want to enter between excess and test. I trade a
1-hour chart, so I do not want to be stuck in a very slow move.

@@b1778 p
Figure: The trade was right but slow: after the target level, price
sat in a narrow box for days before it moved on. Real data: AUDUSD,
1-hour, 24 Feb – 4 Mar 2022. (The original chart was TRYJPY 1-hour,
11–17 August 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b1779 p
Here is the result: the market reached the runaway count. The trade
was profitable but very slow, as the box shows. For 6 days the market
moved in a very narrow range. A waste of time. Our goal is not only a
winning trade; the time must also be reasonable. I cannot call this the
best available trade. When the ME is not directional, it is very hard to
ride a trade.

@@b1782 p

This trade is very far from the bottom, BP-FPS. Very, very risky for
a 1-hour chart. "Manage the risk, let the market manage the reward"
(Goodman). Without making any other judgement, any trade with high risk
must be avoided.

@@b1785 p
Figure: Price went on to a good reward, but the risk at entry was too
high to take. Real data: USDJPY, 1-hour, 23 Feb – 2 Mar 2022. (The
original chart was USDJPY 1-hour, early August 2021; this one shows the
same structure.)

@@b1786 p
It did give a good reward, very close to the runaway count. But the
problem is that it was too risky, so I avoided it, even though it showed
Y &gt; X. A discounted trade is our aim: I want trades where the risk is
least.

I could not call this a 6&amp;6 Setup: there is no FMX or BMX, just a
close from the Under. But in my opinion, if it is supported by a daily
setup, one can ride it, if and only if the risk is least, as shown here.
In this chart there is:

@@b1794 blockquote
<p>1. A Jumbo.</p>
<p>2. More than 12 bars at the energy level.</p>
<p>3. A reversal bar, very close.</p>
<p>4. A strong bullish angle.</p>

@@b1795 p
That is not enough until I find strong bullish evidence. An FMX
normally shows new buyers. Without an FMX, we must find a strong Overlay
that proves there are buyers, one that plays the role of a magnetic
field like the FMX.

@@b1797 p
Figure: Boxes A, B and C at the low: a 1-2-3 that attracts buyers, a
Dagger, and a confirming 1-2-3, with a quick reversal under C. Real
data: GBPJPY, 15-minute, 1–2 Mar 2022. (The original chart was EURCAD
1-hour, around 12 August 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b1798 ul
<li><p><strong>Box A:</strong> a 1-2-3 plays the role of the magnetic
field that causes B.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At B:</strong> the market moves in a Dagger until the
breakout. At B the sellers are losing their passion.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At C:</strong> confirmation. After the Dagger the market
again gives a 1-2-3.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Yellow circles:</strong> when the Matrix dips underneath
box C, a quick reversal shows strong buyers.</p></li>

@@b1799 p
This case must be supported by a daily setup, some of the Nested
Overlay. The range is very small because of the war, with heavy damage
to both buyers and sellers. Every time the market wants to move down, it
gives a 1-2-3, as in A and B, or a Dagger. The stop order goes under the
last up bar, where the risk is least.

I also want to see how the mass is attracted at each of A, B and

@@b1802 p

@@b1803 ul
<li><p><strong>At 1:</strong> with the SPS, the amount of sales

<li><p><strong>At 2:</strong> at the Return Zone, also the beginning of

<li><p><strong>At 3:</strong> the market moves up with almost no energy.
The fight with sellers is very weak, so it moves up easily.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At 4:</strong> the same as 1 at A. Buyers want to enter

<li><p><strong>At 5:</strong> the market moves down to take all the

pushing lower, it gave a very strong close at 5. Buyers dominate the
trend.</p></li>

@@b1804 p
In brief, the mass enjoys the uptrend more than the downtrend. When
the market moves up on increased volume, as at A and B, it shows bullish
passion. Sellers try to stop that passion, but 5 shows there are no more
sellers.
