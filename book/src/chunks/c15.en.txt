@@b955 h3
Cycles govern volume and
price

@@b956 p
I find that the Crosspoint (Angle Propagation) and the Spread Triple
give a clearer picture than volume. Cycles govern volume, and price too.
You may not believe it yet, but the show is just beginning. The market
can always teach us, and it can also shock us.

@@b957 p
The Earth's cycle governs the weather everywhere. It governs the
temperature (its "price"), and it can govern the mass "volume" too.
Rain, flooding, snow, fog and mist, hail storms, smoke from bush and
forest fires, strong winds, extreme cold and heat: all of these can stop
the traffic (the volume) on the road.

@@b958 p
So why does so much volume appear at 50%? Because of the station
point, the Intersection. Price makes volume; cycles make price.

@@b959 p
When a setup fails, we are in bad weather in the market. Enjoy your
stop order and be thankful, because losing capital is the worst idea.
Some think, "I will miss that trade." Even if you do not miss it, you
cannot trade 100 lots, so you miss plenty of profit anyway. Cry for the
big banana you could make with 100 lots, little humble man!

@@b960 h3
Angle Propagation
and the stretched spring

@@b961 p
Back to Angle Propagation. It is Propagation by time: time forces new
buyers to recover old profits quickly. Old buyers feel better because
they won the war. Their first target is to recover what they lost. No
one gives up something without a fight, not even animals.

@@b962 p
When the market is in elastic energy, after the EP-SS it moves
quickly away from the Dagger. Momentum increases, the angle steepens,
and a new impression force takes position. A simple experiment shows
this clearly: the response is like a spring that stretches.

@@b963 p
What I try to do is measure that stretch with the Crosspoint. The
Crosspoint gives me the total result of all energy, volume plus Nash
equilibrium, its maximum, and the next target. By following the
Crosspoint in every 1-2-3, I can see when buyers are weak or strong. It
is an overall indicator. In my opinion, it is the best indicator a
trader can have.

When the sellers' SS stops near the BP-FPS, it shows no energy. It is
the same concept. When it happens, there is no complete cycle, no 1-2-3.
I call it a corrupted cycle.

@@b982 p
Think of pulmonary circulation. For any reason it can stop, and we
know that when it stops it means big trouble. When Mr. Market cannot
give me a down 1-2-3, there is no complete cycle, and that means big
trouble for the sellers. I am happy; I will go with the buyers. No new
lows, no downtrend. What is new?

@@b983 p
The Crosspoint measures how far the down cycle got before it stopped.
It can be used for a second-chance 6&amp;6, when supply is extremely
weak.

@@b985 h3
Spread Triples

@@b986 p
A Spread Triple connects three (or more) Return Points, the EPs of
the SSs, in a Propagation. It is the chain between all the 1-2-3s. It
shows the steps: watch the market in every 3 steps, 3 jumps.

@@b988 p
Figure: The Spread Triple: a line through the Return Points chains
the 1-2-3s. Each jump recharges there; a jump that cannot clear the old
high ends the uptrend.

@@b989 p
In each jump, where is its maximum point, where V = 0? We know that
each EP-SS is a point of elastic potential energy. So it is a simple
connection with the EP and the Crosspoint. Each time the market reaches
an EP-SS, it recharges its energy and tries to jump higher than before,
like a child on a trampoline.

@@b990 p
This shows the quality of the capacitor, the battery. As physics
says: "Charging the capacitor by using potential energy source or
voltage source through a frictional media such as the electrical
resistor will cause losing the electrical energy into friction, since
the current passing to charge the capacitor will meet friction in the
resistance."

@@b991 p
When the market cannot jump higher than the old Propagation, the
uptrend is over. It resumes only when it can jump over the old points.
Each time the market recharges, it suggests some loss of energy.

@@b992 h4
What time really means

@@b993 p
Time is one of the dimensions in physics. Do we use time to
understand what happened in the past? No. We use time to answer
questions about the current moment, the critical point. Ninety percent
of human memory exists because of time.

@@b994 p
When a capacitor loses energy over time, no trend can go on forever.
The biggest gains in history came when no one expected a collapse, as in
2008. In some way, that trend was already losing energy.

@@b995 p
We can build a real sequence of events from these forces. The
sequence is like an action movie, always fighting. Our job is to
understand the movie and know who the hero is, who can save the lady. So
let us watch and decide with confidence who the hero is. Sometimes it is
very hard, as in a mystery like <em>The Da Vinci Code</em>.

@@b1002 h3
Mr. Market's house

@@b1003 p
The market can only go up or down. To give that a structure, we look
at the 1-2-3. What creates the FPS, SS and SPS is potential energy.

@@b1004 p
Mr. Market's house has three rooms: FPS, SS and SPS. By watching the
chart, we can see which room he is in. When he lives in the FPS, we know
the next room is the SS, and that is all.

@@b1005 p
So what do I mean by circulation? It is how Mr. Market flows through
the 1-2-3. We know his state from his old jump and from the Crosspoint:
fast or weak, or extremely strong.
