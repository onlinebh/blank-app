@@b3257 p
After a period of down movement, I observed the quality of the
trend.

@@b3260 p
I read it through mental energy, the energy needed to carry price to
a higher or lower level. At A, buyers' expectation carried price above
A. At the next swing, B, it carried price beyond A. At C, it carried
price higher again. On the downside, the purple arrows show a lack of
downward progress.

@@b3261 p
The impression force from X to Y and then Z had the momentum to
change the angle of speed, giving buyers more advantage. D was a
critical point: sellers had to carry price down there, or the trend
would be in trouble. At D buyers took control and carried price above
the line, into the trading zone C–D. The next critical point is the TP
of CD.

Study 10 (22 November 2021)

@@b3263 p
After a fast, high-volume drop to A, price rebounded to B, around 50%
of the A leg, on increasing volume. Volume should have dried up at
B.

@@b3265 p
Figure: A fall to A on the largest volume, a rally to B and a decline

At C, as a reaction to B, volume dried up and price moved sideways
for a long time. Then, at C, volume increased on the bull side, rising
steadily each time price moved up. This was a new character for buyers.
Whenever price moves above B, that creates a 1-2-3.

Study 12 (late November 2021)

@@b3274 p
Keep it simple, Dad. Keep it simple!

@@b3276 p
Figure: Lower highs and lower lows: sellers in control. Real data:
GBPUSD, 1-hour, 21–24 February 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD,
daily, May – 29 November 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3277 p
This chart shows how the 3-C tracks progress in every 1-2-3 and every
Propagation, in sync with buyers and sellers. It tells us to be
proactive. The 3-C warned that sellers' effort was very close to its
Cancels.

@@b3278 p
In the last few days, new volume came into the market, and the next
bar was an inside bar after that volume. This area is very sensitive. I
was looking for more positive behaviour to support the buyers' picture.
The purpose of being proactive is to stay consistent with the habits of
buyers and sellers, using reasonable, probable evidence.

@@b3279 p
The size of the first 1-2-3 shows sellers were relatively strong. But
in the last 1-2-3, after Propagation, sellers were decreasing their
energy, out of fear of buyers' ability. Sellers losing force does not
mean this is exactly the bottom. But if you can be proactive before a
new trend happens, it is an advantage to wait for what is about to
happen. Building a high-probability setup and avoiding trading errors
make a consistently profitable trader. Stay careful, and you will not
miss the whole new movement.

@@b3280 p
When selling loses its force, it suggests something important:
perhaps the market is preparing for an uptrend.

@@b3282 p
Figure: Good jump, two bad Nash points, and sellers slowing at the
upper line. Real data: GBPUSD, 4-hour, 17 February – 4 March 2022. (The
original chart was GBPUSD, daily, May – 29 November 2021; this one shows

@@b3283 p
The last wave suggested preparation for a recovery, at least a small
one to the Tipping Point. The last small wave, from 17 November, was the
slowest wave, with rising volume. Sellers were weak even with heavy
transactions, and those transactions did not help them. When high volume
comes at the end of a move, be alert: the market will not give a
definitive message.

@@b3284 p
From 16 July until that day, sellers found it hard to make reliable
progress. Watch every new lower low. Some slopes show sellers'
acceleration was very slow, with small Nash. Price was at a critical
point.

Study 13 (early December
2021)

@@b3286 p
In many situations, moving from happiness to sadness or back,
emotions act as resistance that comes from habits and beliefs. Sometimes
a small opinion produces a large change in belief. Since we always deal
with unknowns, we should use all available data to solve them. Trading
is an almost mechanical job, and a sound theory can explain the
unknowns.

@@b3287 p
Here is our unknown, read through the 1-2-3 market map.

@@b3289 p
Figure: Falling legs to a new low: is this EP-SPS? Real data: GBPUSD,
1-hour, 1–4 March 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD, daily, May – 3
December 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3290 p
One of the best things in Goodman is that, instead of confusion and
fighting the market, he makes us clear about each step: what do I see
now, and what do I want to see next. The answer is inside us; the trade
is inside you. It requires introspection. As Plato asked, "…why should
we not calmly and patiently review our own thoughts, and thoroughly
examine and see what these appearances in us really are?"

@@b3291 p
On the 1-2-3 map, the current price was at the EP of the SPS.
Confirming this beyond doubt needs a lot of patience while we wait for a
sign. It is also fun to see and enjoy the market's sync. Let us practice
some introspection and train our brains to read the action of the market
itself.

@@b3292 p
In the last few days, the market had moved into an inside bar of the
abnormal-volume day. Was there further progress down? In the next bars
volume decreased: selling was losing momentum. Compare each smaller down
move; their mark was slower intensity. I cannot emphasize enough the
importance of following the market's changes through the element of
time. So I zoomed into the 1-hour chart.

@@b3294 p
Figure: A narrowing triangle after the spike low, with volume spikes
at the lows. Real data: GBPUSD, 15-minute, 24–25 February 2022. (The
original chart was GBPUSD, 1-hour, 18 November – 6 December 2021; this
one shows the same structure.)

@@b3295 p
The lower line is where buyers emerged to support the market. Volume
shows how much buyers spent, how focused they were, and how they
absorbed sellers' offers. The first arrow was a rebound from the panic
of a wide down bar. At the second, sellers tried for a lower low but
failed to reach the last low. Then the market made higher support on low
volume, and the third attempt tried to break the Dagger line.
