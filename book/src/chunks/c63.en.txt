@@b3698 h1
A Last Letter to Michael

@@b3699 p
Dear Michael,

@@b3700 p
The first letter you wrote me began with "Hello - Thank You!" and a
note that the CODEX was a little out of date. That was September 2020. I
answered that every single word of the Goodmanisms must be memorized by
any serious trader. I did not know then that I would spend six years
learning what those words meant, one chart and one letter at a time.

@@b3701 p
You never explained everything. You did what Charlie did with you:
you showed me a chart, asked a question, and waited. When I sent you
something wrong, you told me. When I sent you something right, you said
Charlie would be pleased. On 2023-03-18 you wrote, "Charlie say,
'Mohamed is Magic Man.'" I laughed, and I kept that line close on the
bad weeks.

@@b3702 p
On 2025-06-22 you wrote the sentence I read more than any other:

@@b3703 blockquote
<p>"Everything Goodman soon yours - Friend, Brother, Son Mohamed!"</p>

@@b3704 p
In the same letter you said that after the article on Abduction and
the Playbook, I would "take over for Charlie and Me." I do not think
anyone takes over for Charlie. But I understood what you were giving me.
Not the books. The responsibility.

@@b3705 p
On 2025-12-20, when I sent you the Power diagrams, you wrote that you
were "almost in tears. ... What a miracle Charlie and I found you!!!!"
Michael, the miracle was the other way. A man on the other side of the
world wrote to a man in Colorado about a theory almost nobody remembers,
and the man in Colorado answered every letter for six years.

@@b3706 p
On 2026-09-28 you wrote:

@@b3707 blockquote
<p>"if it wasn't for you all the stories of charlie would surely be
lost!...i don't know if they will ever inspire anyone else - but at
least they are preserved!"</p>

@@b3708 p
This book is my answer. The stories are not mine. They were yours
first, and Charlie's before that. I only kept them in order. You carried
them for fifty years in your head, the bus station, the old woman's
money, the boxcars that did not buck, the lake and the bathtub. I put
them on the ladder so that whoever climbs it after us can hear Charlie's
voice the way you still hear it.

@@b3709 p
You taught me that the goal is breakeven, that a Setup is set up to
go, that we always leave something for the Line. This book is my Line. I
took the Under over six years of letters. This part I leave running, for
whoever comes next.

@@b3710 p
Thank you, Master.

@@b3711 p
Your friend, brother and son,

@@b3712 p
Mohamed
