Guidelines

@@b1995 p
To summarise into guidelines:

@@b1997 p
Figure: Points A, B and C, where rising volume produced the FPS, the
SS and the SPS. Real data: EURUSD, 15-minute, 24–25 Feb 2022. (The
original chart was EURUSD 1-hour, 6–11 October; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b1998 ul
<li><p><strong>At A:</strong> energy increased relatively, which
produced an FPS.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At B:</strong> increased volume, as the setup shows,
produced an SS.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At C:</strong> volume increased to produce an
SPS.</p></li>

@@b1999 p
To use this in real time, read the behaviour of energy within the
FPS, SS and SPS. Then watch where volume increases but, instead of
moving in the same direction, price shows a tendency to move the
opposite way.

@@b2000 p
<strong>Caution:</strong> do not look for divergence between price
and volume; it is a misleading way to deal with volume. Focus on
decreasing and increasing energy together with the momentum of price.
Whenever there is no momentum, be on alert.

@@b2002 p
Figure: Coloured bands mark how volume collected and spent energy at
A, B, C and the last new high. Real data: EURUSD, 15-minute, 24–25 Feb
2022. (The original chart was EURUSD 1-hour, 6–11 October; this one
shows the same structure.)

@@b2003 ul
<li><p><strong>At A:</strong> energy increased, and price action shows
sufficient buyers to move it higher. The purple line shows price
collecting energy.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At B:</strong> energy increased, but with a tendency to
move lower, as the 1-2-3 shows. The blue line shows the market in the
correction phase of the FPS move.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At C:</strong> energy increased, but the tendency was to
move higher rather than lower, as the nested 1-2-3 setup shows.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>The last yellow line:</strong> an abnormal increase on a
new higher high, then a move to equilibrium on low volume at the last
line.</p></li>

@@b2004 p
Volume can help detect tipping levels when we watch the relative
increase. At B, the energy at level 3 shows buyers unable to move
higher. At C, the energy at level 2 shows sellers unable to move lower.
Both show no momentum to transit to the next level.

Two more examples

@@b2007 p
Figure: Volume points 1, 2 and 3 around the creation of the MO origin
point. Real data: EURUSD, 1-hour, 22 Feb – 1 Mar 2022. (The original
chart was EURUSD 1-hour, 6–11 October; this one shows the same

@@b2008 p
At 1, the down move comes on decreased energy. At 2, energy increases
with a tendency to move higher; this creates the MO origin point. At 3
there is energy, but price moves sideways instead of falling fast as at
1. In the last few bars (blue line) the market moves by its own weight,
on small energy. Whichever energy wins in the sideways move can control
the market.

@@b2010 p
Figure: At X, energy rises and price transits to the next level with
good momentum. Real data: EURUSD, 5-minute, 2 Mar 2022. (The original
chart was EURUSD 5-minute; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b2011 p
Look at X: energy increased and price transited to the next level
with good momentum. Does this behaviour show a tendency to move down, or
up? There is no question: the tendency is up, the SPS.

@@b2013 h2
Volume (2021)

@@b2014 p
In this paper I tell the story of my long struggle with one word:
volume. I want to show how I moved from confusion to a simple, logical
definition I can trade with. At the end I show how I read volume inside
the 1-2-3, with a real EURUSD example.

@@b2015 h3
What I Thought Volume Was

@@b2016 p
I still remember the first time I heard the word in trading. I was
surprised. To me, volume meant sound. I used to work with sound editors
such as Adobe Audition.

@@b2018 p
Figure: A sound wave in an audio editor — the louder the sound, the
wider the wave.

@@b2019 p
Anyone can read such waves easily. When the sound increases, the wave
expands its range. When the sound is very low, the wave narrows. It is
very easy to understand.

@@b2020 p
I am also not a native English speaker. When I played sound on my
computer, my brother would shout at me: "Could you turn the volume down?
I'm trying to sleep." So I set the volume to 20%.

@@b2022 p
Figure: The volume I knew before trading — a slider set to 20.

@@b2023 p
That was everything I knew about "volume".

@@b2024 h3
Meeting Volume in the Market

@@b2025 p
When I came to the market, I felt something bad inside myself:
misunderstanding.

@@b2027 p
Figure: A row of market volume bars — at first it meant nothing to
me. Real data: EURUSD, 1-hour volume, 21–25 February 2022. (The original
was a volume strip cut from another chart; this one is drawn from real
volume.)

@@b2028 p
What on earth was that? The technical books I read made this unknown
thing a top priority. Did anyone tell the truth about volume? I was
greedy. I wanted to know the next direction. I wanted a nice car. Was
the truth hidden somewhere here?

@@b2029 p
But I could not see any of that dream truth. What could I learn from
columns that all looked the same? My aim was to find the next direction,
and my mind kept showing an error. Was this important at all? Perhaps it
was more logical to forget volume and look somewhere else.

@@b2030 h4
Stories without evidence

@@b2031 p
In the following weeks, I found books that spoke about manipulators
hiding in the volume. Wow. But I do not believe anything without
evidence. I am not in court; anyone can make a claim. Without evidence,
I cannot accept it. Even if such a story sank into my unconscious mind,
how could I profit from it?

@@b2032 p
Other books built equations to analyze volume. My answer was: first
tell me what volume is, then play with all the magical equations. They
showed lucky charts with divergence — buy here, sell there. Even if that
game paid me a million, I would not allow myself to play it.

@@b2033 p
Very experienced people created other indicators: the Force Index, On
Balance Volume, and more — delta, gamma and so on. To me these are only
a second translator of volume.

@@b2034 h4
Finding my own path

@@b2035 p
So I tried to find my own path. I do not care about pool traders who
control the market; I am happy for them. You can detect them easily in
very small stocks. It is probably easier to make a deal with them than
to recognize them from volume. But what about Forex, or futures in the
past? There is no central volume. Is there a big ghost controlling the
market? Maybe the big banks.

@@b2036 p
When I found myself in no man's land, that confusion itself told me
something. I needed a path that was more logical and easier — one I
could at least be confident with. So I went back to simple logic:

@@b2037 ul
<li><p>Behind one door, when it opens, there is a lion.</p></li>
<li><p>Behind the other door there is at least a safe path that suits my
ability.</p></li>

@@b2038 p
I am happy for those who can play with indicators and fight the lion.
I will not choose that path. In the end, success in the market means
trading with confidence, and confidence depends on each person's
character — "to each his own".

@@b2039 h3
Expectation Versus Reality

@@b2040 p
Finding your own path takes patience. One of the hardest questions
is: what do you want? The second is: what is the aim behind it? I love
the work of Socrates.

@@b2041 p
At this point I stood between two paths: comfort and reality. The
comfortable path may simply not exist. Sometimes we are happy with a
beautiful but false image, while reality is very far from our
expectation.

@@b2042 p
My first expectations about volume were like my dream car:

@@b2043 blockquote
<p>1. If I understood volume, I could anticipate the next direction.</p>
<p>2. If I knew nothing about volume, I could not trade, because the
truth lives in the volume.</p>
<p>3. My greed could cover my eyes, so I would not believe what the
market says about itself.</p>

@@b2044 p
In this state it is easy to fall and give up. But I could do it.
Anyone who begins to see the facts from outside starts to learn deeply.
Why? Because he finds that what is inside him is unreal. Thank God, our
minds can change as easily as our hands can move, with a little
practice. Otherwise I would stay ignorant forever.

@@b2045 blockquote
<p>"A man should look for what is, and not for what he thinks should
be." — Einstein</p>

@@b2046 h3
The Rule of Signs

@@b2047 p
In simple logic, is there any sign that a path exists? There is a
strong rule: anything that exists has a sign that correlates with it.
Anyone can say he owns a car, but the car must show up somewhere. If the
car is nowhere and there is no sign, it does not exist.

@@b2048 p
For a joke: one student in my school was a big liar. Every day he
claimed he came to school by helicopter. There was never any sign. That
is the mark of a liar — claiming something that does not exist.

@@b2049 p
So I follow this logic: wherever there is a sign, one sign is enough
for me. Let us start the journey to discover a simple path.

@@b2050 blockquote
<p>"It's not that I'm so smart, it's just that I stay with problems
longer." — Albert Einstein</p>
<p>"A thousand roads lead a man forever toward Rome."</p>
