Three
Swiss Franc Trades: When the Overlay Is Not Ready (2025)

@@b2612 p
In this paper I review three short-term trades I took in the Swiss
franc pairs (AUDCHF, EURCHF and USDCHF) in March 2025. Each one starts
from a Jumbo. The charts are redrawn on real 2022 data from other pairs,
named in each caption, with the same structure. The lesson is about the
Overlay: the Setup chart can be clear while the Overlay chart is not
ready yet.

How I Manage a Trade

@@b2654 p
Charlie taught me to read the market. Managing a trade is the part I
had to build for myself, one email at a time. When I look back at what I
wrote to you, Michael, I see the same few ideas coming back, each time a
little cleaner.

@@b2655 h3
The first aim is breakeven

@@b2656 p
In May 2021 I wrote to you about breakeven, and I still stand by
every word: "The importance of BreakEven. if I am right the meanings of
break-even the level trades start to show a profit. if I protect the
breakeven and the market doesn't play against me I will Take The line."
And then the sentence I would put on my wall: "GoodMan this is the first
aim to traders." (May 10, 2021)

@@b2657 p
I used football then, and I still like the picture. Once the trade is
at BE, my goal is safe. The other team cannot score against me. Now I
can attack. My money, my experience, and the price of testing my idea
are all protected.

@@b2658 p
Breakeven is not a target for profit. It is a target for safety. A
trade that reaches BE can no longer hurt the account, and a mind that
knows this can think clearly about the rest.

@@b2659 h3
Nests inside the big play

@@b2660 p
By September 2022 I had added a second layer. I would see a setup on
the hourly or daily chart, but I would enter on a smaller Nest, on the
5-minute chart, often from a Dagger. The quick trade had one job: to pay
for the risk of the big one.

@@b2661 p
I wrote to you after one such trade: "so I entered in 5 min and my
aim was to cover the risk of playing with 1 hour - daily chart. my idea
is simply to play anything that covers the origin risk of BIG PLAY."
(September 20, 2022)

@@b2662 p
This is a simple idea, but it changes how the trade feels. Once the
small trade has paid for the risk of the large one, the large one is
free. I can let it breathe. I am no longer staring at every bar.
