@@b568 h3
Structure indication

@@b569 p
A structure indication is one that has an element of inherence coming
from buyers and sellers. Arranging that element according to the
inherences gives buyers and sellers a structure.

@@b570 h4
The element:
greed, fear, excitement and sadness

@@b571 p
The element comes from human nature. Humans have greed and fear.
These forces give us the intensity to act on an opportunity: the
<strong>when</strong>. They also give us the power to cause action: the
<strong>why</strong>. But they follow one rule: NO EXTREME EXAGGERATIONS
NOR NEGLIGENCE.

@@b572 p
Extremes show the maximum desire of buyers and sellers. The reaction
to this process is excitement. In this game, the opposite of excitement
is sadness.

@@b573 p
Humans are excited when they can hit their goal, and sad when they
cannot. This is the element.

@@b574 h4
Arranging the element by
inherence

@@b575 p
The element is arranged according to the last two inherences:

@@b576 blockquote
<p>1. They show their existence on the chart by deciding
<strong>when</strong> they want to enter orders into the market at any
time.</p>
<p>2. They show their existence on the chart by deciding
<strong>why</strong> they want to enter orders into the market at any
time.</p>

@@b578 p
Figure: One leg of buying told by the Goodman points: the BP where
buyers start, the 50% Tipping Point, and the EP where greed runs
out.

@@b579 h4
The Goodman points tell the
story

@@b580 p
The Goodman points use this element.

@@b581 ul
<li><p><strong>BP (Beginning Point)</strong> is the point that answers
when buyers enter orders. Greed drives the force. Buyers act by entering
orders to absorb the offers. This reflects courage to take the
opportunity, and the excitement of hitting the goal.</p></li>
<li><p>Along the way, buyers do not allow any significant movement in
the opposite direction (25%).</p></li>
<li><p>Greed* then rises to the extreme: EXTREME EXAGGERATIONS. There
the <strong>EP (Ending Point)</strong> occurs. Buyers have exceeded
their ability and cannot continue.</p></li>

@@b582 p
Every Goodman point tells its own story.

@@b583 p
*There is nothing wrong with greed. Here it is simply a force to act,
different from its usual meaning.

@@b584 h3
Types of arrangement

@@b585 p
There are three types of arrangement: Congruence, Implicitly and
Intrinsic.

@@b586 h4
Congruence

@@b587 p
Congruence is an indication that matches the most recent story. It
gives a mirror story, or a high-performance mirror story.

@@b589 p
Figure: GBPUSD, 5-minute, 1–2 March 2022. Buyers drive A to B,
correct to C near the TP, then drive C to D with the same force: CD is a
congruent mirror of AB.

@@b590 p
The story starts at A, when buyers decide to enter orders. Their
maximum comes when they reach point B. They then correct their positions
at mid-way (TP). Then buyers enter again at C and move to D.

@@b591 p
The story CD is a mirror of AB; it is congruent with it. This
arrangement shows that buyers in CD have the same force as in AB. The
trend is up, for simple reasons:

@@b592 ul
<li><p>Buyers can recover their strength from the correction.</p></li>
<li><p>Even after the correction, buyers can move with performance
similar to their past extremes.</p></li>

@@b593 blockquote
<p>"Everything is a 1-2-3."</p>

@@b594 h4
Implicitly

@@b595 p
Implicitly is an indication that is implied by the most recent story.
It gives a sign of something about to occur.

@@b597 p
Figure: Sellers lose force at C and again at E while buyers re-enter;
two implicit signs put the downtrend in doubt and point to the buying
force at F. Real data: USDCZK, daily, 27 October 2021 – 4 March 2022.
(Same symbol, timeframe and dates as the original chart. The daily data
file has no volume, so the volume panel is empty.)

@@b598 p
Buyers enter at A with the force to carry price to the maximum at B,
mid-way of A (TP).

@@b599 ul
<li><p><strong>First implicitly:</strong> sellers at C lose their force.
This shows either that buyers are too strong or that sellers are too
weak. The downtrend is now in doubt.</p></li>
<li><p>Buyers enter again at C and show their force, but there is no
improvement in their performance at D.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>Second implicitly:</strong> sellers lose intensity.
Buyers enter at E with a logical indication, shown in the circle around
the closing of the bars. T is a character indication.</p></li>

@@b600 p
Because the selling force loses its intensity at E (the second
implicitly), a buying force at F is implied. CD then forms a congruence,
a high-performance mirror story, with EF.

@@b601 p
At the first implicitly at C, a buying force is also implied, which
makes DE an inside move within BC.

@@b602 p
Implicitly works as a diagnostic tool to check the intensity of the
trend. Several implicit signs are needed to make the message clear.

@@b603 h4
Intrinsic

@@b604 p
Intrinsic is a compound of Congruence and Implicitly. It is the most
important type of structure indication. It appears naturally as part of
a consequence of indications. The consequence cannot be separated from
the indications.

@@b606 p
Figure: A 1-2-3 nested inside a larger 1-2-3, where A and B are
congruent and C is implicit; price returns toward the TP of the larger
(1). Real data: GBPSGD, daily, 17 June 2021 – 4 March 2022. (Same
symbol, timeframe and dates as the original chart. The daily data file
has no volume, so the volume panel is empty.)

@@b607 p
Every 1-2-3 sits inside a larger 1-2-3. This happens naturally in the
market, and it cannot be separated. The market will follow this
structure.

@@b608 p
Because it is intrinsic, it lets us predict: after a 1-2-3, watch for
a return to the same level as the TP of the larger (1).

@@b609 p
Here A and B are congruent. C is implicit, and there the intrinsic
structure shows the way. The price itself does not matter. Price will
follow and move to the Goodman points: the bounces X, Y and Z.

@@b610 blockquote
<p>"The markets can only go up or down." — Charles B. Goodman</p>

@@b611 h2
How One Can
Construct a Logical Combination (2021)

@@b612 p
I want to show how a trader can build a logical combination instead
of relying on guesses, expectations or tips. A combination built on
logic can set, and keep, a very good return. To get there, I first ask a
simple question: what do we actually mean by trading?

@@b613 p
I develop the engineering-balance argument and the four extreme
combinations fully in Overlay Combination (Rung 4).

@@b615 p
Figure: The trading balance as a process unit, with the three-month
example worked through.

@@b616 p
My job is to qualify the Overlay. When the market is not in one of
these cases, there is no signal. I trade only extremes, as a kind of
nested Overlay, and I use MO and PO to support that nested Overlay.

@@b617 h3
Examples

@@b618 p
Here are four examples, with my observations.

@@b619 h4
Case
1 of 4: a 6x6 on the daily structure (first traded on GBPUSD)

@@b621 p
Figure: A long rise to the MO, a sideways pause below it, then a
small 6x6 climbing back to the MO line, marked as the extreme. Real
data: AUDUSD, 4-hour, 14 February – 2 March 2022. (The original chart
was GBPUSD, daily, July 2020 – May 2021; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b622 p
When there is a lot of propagation in the BMX, it suggests weakness.
Why? The market cannot give me a fast wave. Think of a car that stops
every ten minutes: stop, stop, stop. The same behavior is here. We also
have the MO, which gives me some logical sense about the extreme.

@@b623 p
The market is now at an extreme on the daily chart, as in case 1. Let
us open the 1-hour chart to find the extreme there. There is a PO
Overlay.

@@b625 p
Figure: The PO Overlay: sellers make a lower low under the earlier
low, and the weak pullback that follows is the sell point. Real data:
EURUSD, 1-hour, 16–24 February 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD,
1-hour, May – June 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b626 p
It is better to see a lower low, as the arrow shows. Yes, such a
trade is hard to manage. But any skilled trader can get a good
generation (return) by raising the stop order with each propagation of
the 1-2-3, or by nesting with the daily chart STP.

@@b628 p
Figure: After the sell, the stop (STP) is moved down to each new
lower high as the 1-2-3s propagate. Real data: EURUSD, 1-hour, 21
February – 4 March 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD, 1-hour, 25 May
– 22 June 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b629 p
A good TTL, isn't it?
