##CTX Part: Rung 3 · Seeing: What Is a Market? | Section: The Analysis of Market Mind (2021)

@@b1210 h3
Chart analysis:
reading energy box by box

@@b1211 p
This analysis is purely theoretical. It uses the 1-2-3 and the
Goodman Box with time and volume, going from uptrend to downtrend to
uptrend as one full 1-2-3 cycle.

@@b1213 p
Figure: A first swing and its reaction, with the highest volume of
each swing below.

@@b1214 p
The columns at the bottom represent volume. Simply take the highest
volume within each swing. Volume represents the intensity of belief in
reaching the maximum. What is the next swing?

@@b1215 p
By the Measured Move Rule, the first swing moves to 100%, and the
reaction swings back to 50%.

@@b1216 h4
Which side has greater
energy?

@@b1217 p
You do not need to know what is happening. Our task is to know which
side has greater energy. Then we build a probability logic showing
whether the chance favors the buyers' energy or the sellers'. If we
cannot tell, we are neutral, because the chance is 50% for each
side.

@@b1218 p
In this example the chance is to move to the SPS, for four signs:

@@b1219 blockquote
<p>1. Buyers ended their move at 100%, while sellers ended at 50% of the
buyers' swing.</p>
<p>2. Buyers held the swing (time) longer than sellers.</p>
<p>3. Volume increased on the buyers' wave and decreased on the
sellers'. Buyers show more intensity.</p>
<p>4. The range of the FPS swing, with its volume, is relatively
regular.</p>

@@b1220 p
So the chance of an SPS and a Propagation into an uptrend is more
favorable. When there is no 1-2-3 yet, we place the box neatly around a
swing, until the chart shows a 1-2-3.

@@b1221 p
These four arguments are the premises of the hypothesis that the near
future is Propagation. The hypothesis can be false whenever the chart
does not follow it. The market confirms the hypothesis.

@@b1223 p
Figure: Four signs of energy (range, time, volume and angle),
compared box by box to see which side chance favours.

@@b1224 h4
Box 3: the 1-2-3 completes

@@b1226 p
Figure: The 1-2-3 completes; box 3 gains +3 boxes above the top of
box 1.

@@b1227 p
At this point the market completes the 1-2-3. What is the chance for
the next point? Let us compare the changes in the boxes.

@@b1228 p
In box 3 the volume increased, and price gained more than three
boxes. The range of the swing is relatively regular, but not like the
old behavior in box 1; buyers fell back a little. Box 3 gains +3 boxes,
more than the box 2 reaction, so there is progress on the upswing.

@@b1229 p
Two inferences follow:

@@b1230 blockquote
<p>1. In box 3 buyers slowed a little, judged by the intensity of volume
and by time, comparing boxes 1 and 3.</p>
<p>2. Buyers cancel by the 3-C. To me this does not prove they are very
strong. Strong buyers must renew their energy to make a breakaway 3-C
count. So the buyers are regular, but not that strong.</p>

@@b1231 p
Where there is no intensity from sellers, the chance is only for a
reaction. We redraw a box with the 1-2-3. Watch the chart like a movie:
the energy changes between sellers and buyers, always inside the
Propagation framework.

The chart confirms the last hypothesis. Volume in box 2 is lower than

correction ratio also grows larger.

@@b1237 p
Here the chart shows a buy Setup signal. The chance favors a move to

@@b1238 h4
Mental energy in box 3

@@b1240 p
Figure: Box 3 reaches only +3 above box 1, on a faster angle and the
lowest volume.

@@b1241 p
Let us watch the mental energy in box 3. Buyers seek their maximum
with low intensity; compare it with boxes 1 and 2. Yet that low
intensity produces a good range of swing, a good box. Box 3 gains only
+3 above box 1, but time improves: the angle is faster than in box 1.
(Box 3 can be flat or complex; box 1 was complete, and box 3 here is
flat.)

@@b1242 p
Let me summarize the facts in box 3:

@@b1243 ul
<li><p>The angle is faster; buyers move more quickly than in box
1.</p></li>
<li><p>The range of box 3 is smaller than box 1.</p></li>
<li><p>Volume is lower than in box 1 or box 2.</p></li>

@@b1244 p
Now let us connect these facts to deduce the relative energy. The
mental energy is very fast, the endpoint gains relatively little, and
the intensity is very low.

@@b1245 p
The move is easy (angle) with less intensity (volume). In box 1 the
intensity was high but the angle normal; there was a latent buyer, as
the high volume shows. In box 3 a small volume produces a good range,
which says buyers can rise easily with very low energy. But when I add
the Measured Move Rule, buyers are very slow in progress: just +3, then
the swing ends. This case is a little intricate.

@@b1246 p
To decide it we still need more evidence about floating supply. The
chance to resume the Propagation is lower than in the earlier case of
boxes 1 and 2, where we had the buy Setup. So the next step is the
reaction SS, and we redraw the box and lines.

@@b1247 h4
Box 2 of the new cycle

@@b1249 p

@@b1250 p
In box 2 the angle is very slow, slower than the old SS, and the

@@b1251 p
When sellers cannot move fast, the message is that buyers' orders are
stopping them. It is a tug of war. Whenever sellers are slow, either
buyers are slowing them, or they are slow without any buyers'
orders.

@@b1260 h4

@@b1263 p
In box 2 there is some improvement in the sellers' behavior. From

cannot move above the box. The chart is moving into a Dagger.

@@b1265 p
Figure: The box becomes a Jumbo: the last buyers lose energy while

@@b1266 p
First, the box becomes a Jumbo. In the last box the buyers are losing
energy, while the sellers have new energy in box 2. Let us confirm it
with a 1-2-3.

@@b1268 p
Figure: A down 1-2-3 after the Jumbo turns me from neutral to

@@b1269 p
Whenever I have a 1-2-3 after the Jumbo, I change from neutral to
short. The volume in the Jumbo is cancelled, because this is the new
trend.

@@b1270 p
Be careful at the change from a Jumbo to a new trend: all the volume
relations change. Only look at the higher timeframe, treat the Jumbo as
the FPS and the new trend as the SS. We must always read volume and time
within the trend structure, the Propagation, and the Overlay
combination.

@@b1272 p
