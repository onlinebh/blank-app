the downtrend.

@@b1600 ul
<li><p><strong>At A:</strong> the buyers fail to move up and keep moving
around and around from 2 to 3. This leads us to B. In this box the
relationship shows that sellers control the market, but at a relatively
slow speed. In other words, they react slowly.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At C:</strong> again the buyers fail to move up. They
show some energy, but without success, and lose their power in a
sideways move from 5 to 6. The sellers again move relatively faster than
they did at A.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At E:</strong> the buyers stop at the TP at 8, which
shows no energy at all. Compare the buyers' reactions at A and C with E:
the buyers are very weak. The sellers are smart. They understand the
buyers are weak, so they increase their speed to F at 9.</p></li>
<li><p><strong>At 10:</strong> for the first time the buyers show this
kind of speed. It is a danger alarm that they may develop a new
uptrend.</p></li>
