The tipping point

@@b3646 p
Which loop runs the account? Most days the balancing loop. But
systems have points where a small push changes everything. Catastrophe
theory studies these jumps. Michael and his old partner Jim had worked
with it, and from 2024 it kept coming back in our letters.

@@b3647 p
On 2024-12-13 I wrote:

@@b3648 blockquote
<p>"Catastrophe ... Is a great model to describe the market . I think
all other Thoerys just a description. Looks great only in books."</p>

@@b3649 p
On 2025-05-23 I tried to picture it as two layers, a heavy stone
resting on an egg, and then I connected it to a Goodmanism:

@@b3650 blockquote
<p>"A stable state is exactly part of the nature of the system , while
the catastrophe is outside of the system , destroying the system ... I
think Charlie when he says , a small profit is a loss . to prevent a
catastrophe."</p>

@@b3651 p
The clearest version came on 2025-11-06, in a letter about the
smoothie and tracking:

@@b3652 blockquote
<p>"3c is a point where market conditions are between hope/fear . like a
saddle point, just a little from the curve everything will change.
followed by a propagation being out of equilibrium point . newborn trend
."</p>

@@b3653 p
A saddle point is a place that looks still from one direction and
falls away from another. The 3C is that place in the market: buyers and
sellers in equilibrium, but "will not stay forever." The trader has the
same point inside him. After a run of wins he sits on the saddle between
confidence and greed. One step one way and the balancing loop holds. One
step the other way and the reinforcing loop takes over. The rules of
Rung 6 exist to be obeyed at exactly that moment, when they feel least
necessary.

@@b3654 h3
Robustness

@@b3655 p
A system that works only in good times is not a system. On 2025-02-17
I wrote to Michael from my years as a programmer:

@@b3656 blockquote
<p>"I think the most important feature in any system is Robustness .
When I programming this factor is most important , how my codes stand
very well against the errors / Bugs ! ... The system simply is an
automatic rule or protocol , that organizes the effort that put in the
input to minimum (Simple and fast as possible) at same time increase
from the productively (output)."</p>

@@b3657 p
In the same letter: "What happens to a trader , looks for perfection
, insted we need to think in Systematic approach , make little changes
(enhance) every time . never change the core ."

@@b3658 p
Robustness is what the balancing loop buys. On 2025-04-20 I wrote
that freedom "is a result of embrace the risk . With the vision of long
run , robustness under difficult time. As well the better times." The
core stays the same, the 1-2-3, the thirty souls, the review. The small
things improve. The system bends and does not break.

@@b3659 h3
The ladder is a circle

@@b3660 p
When I put the ten rungs on paper as a loop, the last one led back to
the first. Learning changes the trader, and the changed trader is a new
Being who sees the next chart with new eyes. On 2024-05-06 I wrote, "I
did a feedback loop ." and found it better to add to a winning trade in
re-propagation than to look for a new one. That small change came out of
the loop and went back into it.

@@b3662 p
Figure: The ladder is a circle. The ten rungs read clockwise; the
System feeds back into Being.

@@b3663 p
Charlie said everything is a 1-2-3. A trader is one too. A beginning,
a tipping point, an end, and the end is the beginning of the next.
