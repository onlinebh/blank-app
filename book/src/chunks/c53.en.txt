##CTX Part: Rung 9 · Learning: Who Carries It On? | Section: The GBPUSD Studies (2021–2022)

@@b3301 h3
Study 14 (late November 2021)

@@b3302 p
You can make a fortune in business from a major shift: in technology,
in style, in social interest. The same applies to trading. Anyone who
spots a shift in a major trend and takes advantage of it can earn a good
return. In Goodman, that shift happens at the Return Zone.

@@b3304 p
Figure: After the strong rise, A, B and C bring the price back into
the box. Real data: GBPUSD, 4-hour, 14–23 February 2022. (The original
chart was GBPUSD, daily, March 2020 – 26 November 2021; this one shows
the same structure.)

@@b3305 p
What is the best message the market can give us? Selling increased at
A on rising effort. The recovery at B stopped on volume that was low
relative to A. When I say "relatively", correct poise requires four
elements.

@@b3306 p
At C, volume increased and price moved down relatively slowly. That
reflects the effect of the Return Zone box. The market was saying that,
at least in the near term, we should avoid selling in the Return
Zone.

@@b3307 p
The attraction of selling from B to C increased. But instead of
helping momentum, which represents the strength of conviction in their
belief, C suggested conflict in sellers' beliefs: all that volume
produced less momentum. Look at the narrow ranges of the last days.

Study 15 (9 December 2021)

@@b3309 p
After a 1-2-3 down with a 3-C Cancel, price was underneath the level
of Intersection.

@@b3311 p
Figure: The line holds, is cancelled, and the swings a–f decide
around it. Real data: GBPUSD, 5-minute, 24 February 2022. (The original
chart was GBPUSD, 4-hour, 29 November – 9 December 2021; this one shows
the same structure.)

@@b3312 p
Energy poised under the level represents a struggle between sellers
and buyers.

@@b3313 blockquote
<p>1. <strong>Bar a:</strong> a first attempt to rally, on increased
volume.</p>
<p>2. <strong>Bar b:</strong> a higher low, closing at its high. For the
moment buyers seemed in control, and the next bars should have
rallied.</p>
<p>3. <strong>The reaction to b:</strong> sellers took the chance to
push lower and erased all buyers' effort, on low volume. There were not
enough buyers to stop it. Buyers simply withdrew.</p>
<p>4. <strong>d and e:</strong> attempts to rally moved price into the
Dagger. The last effort, at f, was an outside bar that closed lower than
the previous bar, a bad reaction.</p>

@@b3314 p
All the struggle at bar a was only profit-taking. It could not
attract more strength, as the low volume from c to f shows. Look out for
a healthier struggle in this area. So far there was not enough buying
energy to carry price up. The Cancel was still active, but it needed
more energy.

Study 17 (mid-December 2021)

@@b3316 p
After a long time, you realize that opportunity in trading never
means waiting for it to knock on your door. What you can see is whether
something is an opportunity, or whether one is on its way to you. "What
do I see now, what do I want to see next."

@@b3318 p
Figure: Sellers slow down and keep testing the same floor. Real data:
GBPUSD, 30-minute, 25–28 February 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD,
4-hour, 18 November – 16 December 2021; this one shows the same
structure.)

@@b3319 p
For several days the chart moved in conflict, with buyers from
different times buying at this price level. A simple, logical rule
applies: no new low, no downtrend. When sellers take days to move within
a small range, the message is that they are losing their belief and
emotional value, and buyers are growing more courageous to take the
trend higher.

Study 18 (17 December 2021)

@@b3321 p
One difficulty traders struggle with is confusion about the market's
action. In the end, a successful trader cannot live with doubt. Here is
an example of how the Goodman method gives light in the dark and makes
everything clear.

@@b3323 p
Figure: From the 50% Tipping Point of the fall, through SS at the low
line, to A. Real data: GBPUSD, 15-minute, 1–2 March 2022. (The original
chart was GBPUSD, 4-hour, 17 November – 17 December 2021; this one shows
the same structure.)

@@b3324 p
After I forecast an up move in the last report, price stopped at A,
at the last TP. Then it moved down between the TP and SS.

@@b3325 p
"When" is a very useful word in trading. When buyers appear between
the TP of A and the SS, I can consider the uptrend resumed. If buyers
disappear and break the line, a different case applies. Until then, the
movie was on the bullish side. Let the market decide the direction; wait
and watch. That is the best strategy with the market.

@@b3326 p
One may ask why I did not treat XX as a full reaction, with its own
SS. In my opinion, sellers were exhausted at SS, and XX was part of
volatility that only supported buyers in the SPS. On my original 4-hour
chart, look at the close of the XX bar: it closed near its high after
dipping below SS. That confirmed how the SS point gives momentum to
buying. When the ball falls, the SS gives it the force to bounce up
again.

Study 20 (23 December 2021)

@@b3328 p
That day buyers took price to the next level: after a V-shaped low
they made a higher low and climbed back to the top line, exceeding the
breakaway count on my original chart. Buyers were very strong and
dominated the trend.

@@b3330 p
Figure: A V-shaped low and the climb back to the top line. Real data:
GBPUSD, 30-minute, 14–16 February 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD,
1-hour, 16–23 December 2021; this one shows the same structure.)

@@b3331 p
This behaviour by buyers was unique for several days, clear evidence
of their ability to propagate into a larger Propagation. In Goodman, we
always track the progress of buyers and sellers in Propagation. Every
line shows how buyers move.

Study 21 (23 December 2021)

@@b3333 p
That day buyers propagated more and more.

@@b3335 p
Figure: A second V: fall, first rally, higher low, and the drive

@@b3336 p
Whenever price goes up, it must come down a little for a reaction;
the market moves by 1-2-3. I was looking for the next reaction, or, if
buyers were very strong, at least sideways movement. Volume was still
normal. You can draw a smaller 1-2-3, as in yellow, to anticipate where
buyers will start losing energy.

Study 22 (27 December 2021)

@@b3338 p
Buyers had been struggling with sellers around the 1.3372 level for
several days, in a sideways movement. But even in sideways movement
there are up and down waves on a lower timeframe, here the 15-minute
chart.

@@b3340 p
Figure: The return zones of the larger matrix (rust) and the smaller
matrix (green). Real data: GBPUSD, 15-minute, 25 February 2022. (The
original chart was GBPUSD, 15-minute, 23–27 December 2021; this one
shows the same structure.)

@@b3341 blockquote
<p>1. In the red box, a 1-2-3 showed buyers unwilling to take sellers'
offers on high volume.</p>
<p>2. Buyers then moved into a smaller 1-2-3, the green box, which in
Propagation is the Return Zone of the red box.</p>
<p>3. When sellers came again, they lost force on low volume, as the
green box shows.</p>
<p>4. The market moved into a Dagger at the green box's Return Zone, on
low volume. Buyers stopped the selling energy with only a small amount
of sales. In Goodman, this is a Knot.</p>

@@b3342 p
For now the Matrix belonged to buyers, until sellers showed their
energy again at a higher level. This happened because the market had
moved to a new high and buyers came to buy more. The heavy selling in
the red-box 1-2-3 was not enough to dominate the Matrix in the green
box.

@@b3343 p
Don't look at bars. Look at lines and Propagations.

@@b3345 p
On the daily chart (redrawn here on 4-hour data), after a Jumbo down,

@@b3347 p
Figure: The Tipping Point of the fall, a first high, a higher low and
the return to that high. Real data: GBPUSD, 4-hour, 21 February – 1
March 2022. (The original chart was GBPUSD, daily; this one shows the

@@b3348 p
In Goodman, the TP represents 50% of buyers' profit and 50% of
sellers' lost profit. It is a very critical point for both sides. In the
last five days of the original chart, buyers were losing momentum,
moving slowly in a very narrow range in front of the TP. Any sharp
reaction from sellers would be a final alert. Buyers had to increase
their force to hold their gains.
